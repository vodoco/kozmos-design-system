# Stepper

Shows progress through a labelled sequence, marking earlier steps complete and one step current.
`currentStep` is zero-based. On the web the steps are a list named by the position ("Step 2 of 5",
from `positionLabel`), each step says its status after its label ("Levels, Done"), and the current
one is `aria-current="step"`; in VoiceOver and TalkBack the current step is selected, the platform's
own word, and each step is read as one item, its number and name together. It does no form validation:
a step's status is the product's to say.

## Dots

A tour shows its progress as dots: `variant="dots"` draws one dot per step and no label. Done
dots are filled with the theme fill, the current one is the long one (20 by 8), so its shape says
it as well as its colour, and pending ones are foreground 500, 3:1 or more against the page, a
popover and the muted fill in both themes. In forced colours, done and current are the system's
Highlight and pending ones an outline.

Put a tour's dots on the page, a popover or the muted fill: those are the surfaces they are
measured on. On background-200 (`bg-secondary`) they fall under 3:1: the light theme's pending
dots read 2.59:1 there, and the dark theme's done and current ones 2.32:1.

The dots are a group whose name is the position, "Step 2 of 5" by default: give `positionLabel`
the product's words, as a function of the step (from 1) and the count, or as a template with
`{step}` and `{count}`. Each dot is a list item named by its step, and the current one is
`aria-current="step"`, as in the numbered stepper.

`variant` is the indicator's style alone. The dots take a step object for its label and draw the
position only: a status, `orientation` and `onStepSelect` are the numbered variant's.

## A status for each step

A step is a string, its label, or an object: `{ label, status?, disabled? }` (GAP-173, decision 119).
Without a `status` a step follows `currentStep`, done before it, current at it and `pending` after
it; with one it is drawn and said as it says. `error` is the danger fill with a cross, as a danger
Button is, the counterpart of a done step's theme fill and check; `loading` is the current ring with
an arc that turns while the step works, and stands still under reduced motion, the media query's or
the design config's. Every status has its own glyph or number, so none is told by colour alone, and
a screen reader hears it in words after the label: "Done", "Not started", "Error" and "In progress"
by default, from `statusLabels` in the product's words. The current step's word is never said: it is
`aria-current="step"`. The fills and rings are 3:1 or more on the page in both themes.

## Vertical

`orientation="vertical"` puts the steps in a column, each indicator 12 before its label and a line
between them, as a wizard's side panel lists them. The label wraps rather than truncating. Right to
left, the indicators sit at the inline start, on the right.

## Going back

With `onStepSelect`, every step the visitor may go to is a button that calls it with the step's
index. By default those are the steps before the current one; a step's `disabled` says otherwise,
`true` to close an earlier step and `false` to open one ahead, as a step already visited is. The
current step is never a button.

Each button is a Tab stop of its own, in order, as Pagination's pages and Breadcrumb's links are.
Kozmos gives one Tab stop and the arrow keys to composite widgets whose role promises them, Tabs,
RadioGroup and SegmentedControl; a list of buttons is not one, so a screen reader user expects Tab
here, and a wizard's handful of steps costs little to Tab through. A step's target is the density's
control height, 44 in touch and 32 in compact, or the step as drawn where that is larger, so a
button moves nothing.

        KozmosStepper(

            currentStep: 1
        )
    }
}

    KozmosStepper(
        steps = listOf("Choose destination", "Review route", "Navigate"),
