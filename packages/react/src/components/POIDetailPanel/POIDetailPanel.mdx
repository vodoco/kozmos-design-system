# POIDetailPanel

Typed Product/SDK detail anatomy for partial and complete POI data. Actions are
controlled and receive explicit loading, disabled, pressed, status, and error
states. The same content contract supports inline, sheet, and desktop panel
presentations.

## SDK reference examples

The **Product SDK / POI Detail Examples** stories rebuild the supplied restaurant,
entrance, retail, fitness and parking references with Kozmos tokens. These five
venue examples, Full Field Catalogue, On Map, Missing Data, Failed Media,
Action States and Long Content are separate acceptance cases. Fixture actions
never call a booking service, share API or routing engine.

The optional `details: POIDetailsPresentation` model adds typed summary items,
ordered information groups, venue-local opening-hours rows, plain-text description
expansion and tags. Empty groups and absent photos/logos are omitted, not replaced
with invented data. The host supplies localized text and explicit availability.
Keep rich API content sanitization, venue timezone handling and opening-status
calculation in the product adapter.

Existing `onAction` and `actionLabels` remain unchanged. Optional book/call
capabilities use `details.supplementaryActions`, `onSupplementaryAction` and
`supplementaryActionStates`; without a callback those actions are disabled.
Favourite/bookmark are controlled toggle buttons in the header. The name and those buttons
share one row whatever the name's length: a long name wraps beside them, three lines at most,
then truncates, and never pushes the buttons under it — the same rule on iOS and Android. The
`LongContent` example carries a name that wraps, and the browser check measures it.

Selected favourites/bookmarks use the themed filled Button treatment with an
outline Pointr icon; unselected controls remain outlined. Navigation uses a
24px icon and 16px/24px semibold label, with the existing ~11px/14px token for
the estimate. Information chips/tags are non-interactive, with a 32px minimum
height, ~13px/16px type, 16px corners and room to grow for wrapped/zoomed text.
They are not 44px action targets.

Group items and services accept optional `iconName`, using the existing Kozmos
icon registry. Icons are decorative, 16px, and explicitly assigned by the adapter;
the component does not infer them from translated text. Unknown icons leave
the full label visible. No external image URL or raw SVG is accepted by this field.

Summary facts use the shared `MetaStrip`: exactly one row of cells, with local
horizontal scrolling when the host is too narrow. Facts never move to a second
row. Labels may wrap within a cell without splitting ordinary words; icon/text
groups remain centered and their text is start-aligned. The two primary-action
text lines share the same starting edge. No font shrinking or hidden facts.

Changing `poi.id` resets panel scroll and description expansion; refreshing the
same POI preserves its scroll position. A failed logo is omitted, retaining the
text title; changing its source retries loading. The host still owns selection
state and focus. Localize `mediaControlsLabel` along with the gallery's previous,
next, position and unavailable labels. Gallery state follows native scrolling,
keyboard steps and accepted controlled values; its alignment does not scroll
the containing panel.

The example uses `AdaptiveMapShell`, not a modal drawer. On narrow hosts an
explicit button requests more detail space; wider hosts use a side panel.
This is not drag/snap interaction or an actual map renderer. Close returns focus
to the example's opener. The application owns focus restoration in real use.

### Intentional differences from the screenshots

- POI surfaces, chips, media and controls use the shared 16px control-radius token.
  Icon buttons are rounded squares, not circles. Edge-attached sheets retain
  square bottom corners.
- Secondary actions wrap rather than becoming clipped, undiscoverable controls.
- Touch targets retain the shared 44px minimum; the header keeps one row and lets the name
  wrap instead.
- No counterfeit logos, payment trademarks, photographs or map tiles are drawn.
- Attribute chips are information, not non-functional buttons.
- Opening hours follow the attribute groups consistently in this first model.
- Estimates, hours, ratings and accessibility claims are illustrative fixture data.

Run `pnpm test:poi-details` against a running Storybook. Set
`ADAPTIVE_BROWSER=firefox` or `ADAPTIVE_BROWSER=webkit` to check another engine.
Screenshots, failures and incomplete accessibility checks are saved under
`test-results/poi-detail-examples/`. This is UI acceptance, not SDK certification.

## In a sheet

`presentation="sheet"` paints no surface, border or shadow of its own: the panel sits on the sheet's, as the browse panel does, and its inset blocks (restrictions, action messages) turn white on that grey.

## Hosted in the map shell

In `AdaptiveMapShell`'s panel, sheet or side panel alike, use `presentation="sheet"`: the card paints
no surface of its own and sits on the panel's. The panel already leaves space above its content —
the grip's 16px row on a sheet, 16px at the top of a side panel — and the header tops its top padding
up to 16 instead of adding another 16, so the close button sits as far from the panel's top as from
its side: 17px and 17px, the panel's 1px border counted (GAP-083: it sat 33px down and 17px in).

Under a grip that holds while the header's buttons stay clear of the grip's target (decision 51).
The grip's 16px row is an undersized target, and WCAG 2.5.8 keeps a 24px circle on its centre clear
of every other target. Flush under the row, the buttons' top edge is 8px below that centre, so the
button nearest the middle must start about 9px past it. Where it would not, the header keeps the
grip's 4px clearance, as every other part at the top of the panel does (decision 14): 21px and 17px.
With favourite, save and close that is a panel under 340px wide (a 320px phone); with one toggle and
close, under 240px; with close alone, under 140px. The header decides by a container query on the
panel's width (`kozmos-panel`), so it follows the panel, not the viewport. iOS and Android draw the
same rule, 16 and 16 or 20 and 16 with no border; on iOS the header measures its buttons, which grow
with the text size, so at the largest sizes it keeps the clearance on wider phones too.

With a single detent there is no grip, and under a `panelHeader` the header sits above the card; in
both the card keeps its own 16.

In the `sheet` presentation the header's buttons draw their keyboard focus ring inside their edge,
as the actions strip draws its own: flush against the card's top, in a side panel and under a grip,
a ring drawn outside lost its top edge to the card's and the panel's scrolling boxes.

`presentation="panel"` and `"inline"` draw a bordered card of their own; the space above that border
is not the card's, so the header keeps its 16 inside it. A card placed lower in the panel than its
top should use `"inline"`.

The shell says what it leaves through two custom properties on its panel's content,
`--kozmos-panel-inset-top` and `--kozmos-panel-clearance-top`, and how wide that content is through
its container, `kozmos-panel` (see AdaptiveMapShell). The rule assumes the card spans the panel's
width, as it does when it is the panel's content: a card inside a padded wrapper sits nearer the
grip's centre than the panel's width says.

On a glass panel the `sheet` presentation's muted lines, its level and hours, its prices, its
summaries' notes, its section headings and its gallery's position, take the foreground colour, so
they read at 4.5:1 over any map (decision 48): the level line read 3.7:1 muted over a saturated map.
The `panel` and `inline` presentations are a card of their own and say so, so their text stays
muted even on a glass panel. iOS and Android do the same; on iOS the opening hours' note follows too,
and on Android the description, both muted there.

In the shell's panel the summary strip paints no fill of its own (decision 43): across a glass sheet
it was an opaque band. On its own it keeps it. iOS's strip paints none anywhere.
