## Boxed

`variant="boxed"` draws each section as a box of its own, for a form in steps: a hairline while closed, a fill of theme 900 while open, and the section's content in the panel's flow under its header. Theme 900 turns over in the dark, where it is pale with a black title. Sections sit 8 apart. A header is the density's control height, 40 at compact and 44 at touch, so a pointer-first tool sets `density="compact"` and a touch product keeps its 44 target. Corners are the control radius.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={AccordionStories.Boxed} />
</Canvas>

- **What is yours.** Which section opens when, the "Step 1/5" words, the Continue and Save buttons and whatever sits beside the panel. There is no built-in step count: `label` is your string, read before the title as part of the trigger's name.
- **Focus at Continue is yours too.** A Continue button sits inside the section it closes, so on a keyboard focus falls to the page and the next Tab starts from the top. Accordion can't know where you're going, so move focus yourself: `AccordionTrigger` forwards its ref, and a trigger can take focus once its section is enabled. The story above commits the state with `flushSync`, then focuses the next trigger.
- **Disabled.** `disabled` on `AccordionItem`, as in the default form. It is greyed, draws no chevron, takes no press or focus, and the arrow keys and Tab pass over it.
- **Loading.** `loading` on `AccordionTrigger` draws a spinner where the chevron is, and marks the trigger busy to assistive technology and disabled until you clear it, in either form. A `disabled` prop on the trigger can't turn that off. SwiftUI and Compose have no busy state to set, so they say `loadingLabel` instead, "Loading" unless you pass your own.
- **`icon` and `label`** are the boxed form's: the default form draws neither.
- **In the content.** It doesn't clip at rest, so a field's focus ring draws whole. Leave room for the ring at the panel's edge: a panel with `overflow: hidden` right at the sections' edge will still clip it.
- **Contrast.** The title and the label are foreground 1000 and theme 300 on theme 900. Each pair turns over with the theme and clears 4.5:1 in both (13.25 and 5.29 light, 14.84 and 5.66 dark). A client theme that replaces theme 300 or 900 takes on that pair: nothing at runtime checks it.
- **Re-branding.** Overriding theme 500 to 700 leaves 900 alone, so a client's open header stays Pointr's navy until you set 900 and 300 too. Set them per theme, with `tokens={{ light, dark }}` on `ThemeProvider`: a flat set puts a dark 900 under the dark theme's black title, about 2:1.
