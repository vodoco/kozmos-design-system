# Navbar

Horizontal app navigation shell. Compose route items with `NavigationItem` inside the `navigation` slot, and pass product context through named slots such as `logo`, `context`, `primaryAction`, `actions`, `utilities`, and `account`.

The shell wraps according to its available width, including inside a narrow pane
on a large screen. Its minimum height is 64px, not a fixed height: context, actions,
and navigation remain available rather than disappearing at a breakpoint. Allow
the host layout to grow with it. Consumer-provided navigation groups should wrap
and should not impose fixed widths larger than their container. `navigationLabel`
names the navigation landmark (default: “Main navigation”).

## Where the navigation sits

`navigationAlign` places the navigation in the room between the leading slots (logo, context, primary
action) and the trailing ones (actions, utilities, account). `"center"`, the default, centres it, as
the bar has always drawn it; `"start"` sets it right after the logo; `"end"` beside the account, as
Pointr Cloud draws its main sections (GAP-196). Start and end are logical: in
right to left, `"end"` is the left. When the bar wraps, the navigation keeps its alignment on its
own line. The landmark, its name and the reading and tab order stay as they are: only where the items
are drawn changes.

A Home tile belongs in the `logo` slot, as an icon-only top `NavigationItem`, not in `actions`, which
sits in the group at the bar's end.

SwiftUI's and Compose's `KozmosNavbar` take it too, as `navigationAlign: .start`, `.center` or `.end`
(`KozmosNavbarNavigationAlign`) and `navigationAlign = KozmosNavbarNavigationAlign.End`, `.center` by
default, with the same room and the same mirroring right to left.

## Over a rail

`logoInset` sets the logo in a column as wide as Sidebar's rail, from the bar's edge (GAP-208). With a
rail under the bar at the page's start, a Home tile sits over the rail's items, and what follows
starts past the rail, so a block 424 wide ends where a 440 panel beside the rail does. Pointr Cloud
draws its shell this way. It takes no length, because the only right width is the rail's. Left out,
the logo sits at the bar's padding and is as wide as it is. The column is logical: in right to left it
is at the bar's right, over a rail there. SwiftUI's and Compose's `KozmosNavbar` take `logoInset` too.
