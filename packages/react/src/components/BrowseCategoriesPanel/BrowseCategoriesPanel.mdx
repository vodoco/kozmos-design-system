# BrowseCategoriesPanel

Scrollable Product/SDK category composition. Search and actions remain ahead of
the category grid in DOM and keyboard order, while safe-area padding keeps the
last row reachable on mobile.

## A category's colours

`tint` returns a `CategoryTint` per category — `{ accent, fill, onFill }` — and each tile wears it; leave it out for the theme's colour.

## In the map shell's panel

As the whole content of `AdaptiveMapShell`'s panel, the browser is the panel's top. Its first row
(the search row, or the tiles when there is no search row) tops its 16px up to what the panel
already leaves above it, rather than adding 16px to it. Under a sheet's grip it keeps 4px more, so
no tile or field sits inside the grip's target circle (WCAG 2.5.8). The search field used to sit
33px from the panel's top and 17px from its side; it now sits 21px down under a grip, and 17px down
in a side panel or a sheet with a single detent. The tiles under a search row keep their 16px.
On its own, outside a shell, nothing changes.

If the search field lives in the shell's `panelHeader`, leave out `search` here: the browser's tiles
then sit 16px under the header. If you place the browser under a row of your own inside the panel,
set `--kozmos-panel-inset-top` and `--kozmos-panel-clearance-top` to `0px` on it, because it is not
at the panel's top. On iOS, host it with `presentation: .sheet`.

In the shell's panel, a sheet or a side panel, the browser paints no fill of its own: the panel's
surface, solid or glass, is the one surface (decision 43). On a glass panel the map shows through it
where it stood as a white block; on a solid one it looks as it did, and its text keeps the theme's
foreground. On its own it keeps the background colour. iOS paints none in the `sheet` presentation,
and Android none at all. On glass its empty state's text, muted elsewhere, takes the foreground
colour, so it reads at 4.5:1 over any map (decision 48).
