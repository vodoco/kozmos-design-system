# FloorSelector

Displays the FloorSelector interface topology natively.

## Variants

`vertical-list`, `horizontal-list`, `compact-stepper` and `collapsible` exist on every platform.

`collapsible` is the SDK's level switcher, for a control parked in a corner of a map. At rest it is
one map control showing the current level's short label. Activated, it grows into a column of every
level over itself, top floor first, each level its short label as the tile shows it, the current
one outlined in the theme's primary and a closed level muted. A choice, Escape or a tap outside
closes the column, and focus goes back to the tile, which names the level now shown. The tile is
the map's own control (`MapControlButton`, `KozmosMapControlButton` on iOS and Android), so it
follows the shared map-control surface; screen readers hear whether its column is open.

<Canvas of={FloorSelectorStories.Collapsible} />

## The visitor's level

Pass the level the visitor is on as `userFloor`, by the same id as `selectedFloor`, and the
switcher marks it with a dot, as the SDK's does: on the closed tile while the tile shows that
level, and on that level in the open column whichever level is shown. It is said with the level's
name — "Level 1, your level" — in the product's words, `userFloorLabel`. Leave it out and nothing is
marked. The product knows where the visitor is; the switcher neither works it out nor chooses a
level by it. Only `collapsible` draws the dot. In its column a level's result count sits at the
bottom corner, the top one being the dot's, so the two never cover each other.

## The order of the levels

List the levels **top floor first**, on every variant: the highest level first and the basements
last, as on a lift panel and in the SDK's level switcher. The lists draw `floors` in the order you
pass it, and the stepper steps through it in that order, so one top-first list serves every
variant.

## Which level to start on

Start on the venue's **default level** (a property on Pointr's levels); when there is none, start on
the **ground level**. That choice is the product's: FloorSelector is controlled, so pass the level
you chose as `selectedFloor` and move it in `onFloorSelect`. Kozmos never picks a level itself.

## The stepper's buttons

The compact stepper's two buttons are called "Floor up" and "Floor down" on every platform, for a
visitor who cannot see them. The up chevron steps to the previous level in `floors` and the down
chevron to the next, so list the levels top first and up goes up. A product that translates passes
its own words as `previousFloorLabel` and `nextFloorLabel`, the same names on iOS and Android.

}

      selectedFloor={floor}

  );
}
