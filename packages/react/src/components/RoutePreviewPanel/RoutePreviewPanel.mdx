# RoutePreviewPanel

Route calculation and alternative-selection composition. The horizontal list
uses snap points and a localized count cue; continuation is disabled until a
selected route is ready and available.

Outside `ready`, previous options may remain visible when `statusContent` is omitted, but cannot be selected. Supply localized status content to explain calculation or failure; it replaces the options and is announced as a status or alert. Neither an available old option nor omitted status content makes a route ready.

## In the map shell's panel

As the whole content of `AdaptiveMapShell`'s panel, the route preview is the panel's top. Its
destination row tops its 16px up to what the panel already leaves above it, rather than adding 16px
to it. Under a sheet's grip it keeps 4px more, so nothing in the row sits inside the grip's target
circle (WCAG 2.5.8). Under a grip and in a side panel, "To" used to sit 33px from the panel's top and
17px from its side; it now sits 21px down under a grip and 17px down in a side panel, as it already
did in a sheet with a single detent. The options under the row keep their 16px. On its own, outside a
shell, nothing changes. `AdaptiveMapShell`'s `SheetWithRoutePreview` story shows it under a grip.

If you place the preview under a row of your own inside the panel, it is not at the panel's top: move
that row into the shell's `panelHeader`, or set `--kozmos-panel-inset-top` and
`--kozmos-panel-clearance-top` to `0px` on the preview.

iOS and Android read the same two values from the shell: `kozmosPanelInsetTop` and
`kozmosPanelClearanceTop` in the SwiftUI environment, `LocalKozmosPanelInsetTop` and
`LocalKozmosPanelClearanceTop` in Compose. Under the grab handle "To" sits 20 points (dp) down and 16
in; a native side panel leaves nothing above its content, so there the row keeps its 16. For a
preview under a row of your own, set both values to zero.

In the shell's panel, a sheet or a side panel, the preview paints no fill of its own: the panel's
surface, solid or glass, is the one surface (decision 43). On a glass panel the map shows through it
where it stood as a white block; on a solid one it looks as it did, and its text keeps the theme's
foreground. On its own it keeps the background colour. iOS and Android read the panel's surface
from the shell (`kozmosPanelSurface`, `LocalKozmosPanelSurface`); on iOS the preview's square fill
also covered the panel's rounded top corners.

On glass its muted text, "To", the count of options and the status box's text, takes the
foreground colour, so it reads at 4.5:1 over any map (decision 48). Muted over a saturated map,
"To" read 3.7:1 on the web and under 3:1 on iOS and Android; it now reads 8.5:1 or more on all
three. The route options are cards of their own and keep their muted text.
