# SpriteIcon

One cell of a published MapLibre sprite atlas, drawn as published (GAP-150, decisions 86 and 219): a taxonomy type's
full-colour bubble, its glyph and its marker dot, exactly as the map draws it, never tinted or recoloured. The UI's own
glyphs are Kozmos's `Icon`; a type's icon comes from the published taxonomy sprite, and SpriteIcon is how a content tree,
a type picker or a persona list draws one.

<Canvas of={SpriteIconStories.Default} />

## Anatomy

- **`sprite`** is the atlas's base URL, the one the product's map style names, pinned to its published version, such as
  `https://mapscdn.pointr.tech/sprite/10.17.0/light/sprite`. MapLibre's suffixes are added to it: `.json` and `.png`,
  or `@2x.json` and `@2x.png` on a 2x screen. Kozmos names no version: the product passes the one its map uses.
- **`name`** is the cell's name in the atlas's index, such as a type's subType (`desk`, `atm`).
- **`size`** is the box, 16, 20 or 24 (the default). The cell is scaled into it with its aspect ratio kept: a square
  bubble fills it, a taller marker cell is as tall as the box and narrower.
- **While the atlas loads, or where the cell is missing,** the box stays, empty, at its size, so nothing around it moves.

Each atlas is read once per URL and scale and shared by every icon on the page.

## Accessibility

Decorative unless `label` names it: without a label it is hidden from assistive technology, as a type's icon beside the
type's name should be; with one it is an image named by the label. SwiftUI's `KozmosSpriteIcon` is hidden from VoiceOver,
or one image element named by its `label`; Compose's has no node, or an image node named by its `label`.
