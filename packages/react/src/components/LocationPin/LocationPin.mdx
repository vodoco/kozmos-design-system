## Numbered pins: quiet at rest, filled when selected

A pin with a `number` pairs with its result card's number tab (decision 55). At rest it is quiet: the
outlined marker on the background, with its ring and number in the pin's colour, as the SDK's map
draws its unselected results. Only the selected pin is filled, with the ink made for that fill on
the number, and it grows, so selection is never told by colour alone. Its card's tab fills with it.

- **Keep their fill:** a featured pin (a featured result's pin shows its logo on the map, so a
  product numbers only the others), a pin showing `markerContent`, and a pin with no number.
- **Other variants** keep their colours in the ring and number, as ink that reads 4.5:1 on the
  background in both themes: `secondary`, whose own colour on the web is a surface grey, takes the
  muted foreground. On iOS and Compose, whose theme/500 reads 3.74:1 on the dark background,
  `primary` takes the theme's text role and `accent` its theme variant's 700.
- **Contrast:** the primary number reads 6.24:1 on the light background and 6.17:1 on the dark.
  Its ring reads at least 4.68:1 against Pointr's light map (background, rooms, retail and food)
  and 3.27:1 against the dark map's background, rooms, corridors and retail; dark food-and-drink
  rooms are the exception, at 2.76:1, as the filled pin is.

## On another floor

`offFloor` keeps the outlined marker, with a **dashed** ring and the number in the foreground. The
dashes are what tell it from a quiet pin at rest on this floor, which is outlined too: shape, not
colour, so it holds in forced colours and for any colour vision. Selected, it grows and is never
filled.

## A category's colours

A `tint` — `{ accent, fill, onFill }` — colours the marker with the category's fill; a featured pin
keeps the alert colour. Selected, the fill is the marker and its ink the number. At rest a numbered
pin is outlined in the fill, and off the floor its outline is dashed; in both the number takes the
foreground, since six of the eight fills fail 4.5:1 as text on the background (yellow reads
1.92:1). The same holds on iOS and Compose.
