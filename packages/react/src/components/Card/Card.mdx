## Header actions

`actions` on `CardHeader` puts what acts on the card at the header's inline end (GAP-169): an edit
IconButton beside the site's name, a menu. The title and description keep the room before it and
their own spacing, and the actions are centred on the title's line, with no extra height for an
icon button at either density. In right to left the actions are at the left. They come after the
title in the reading and tab order. Without `actions`, the header is drawn as it was.
