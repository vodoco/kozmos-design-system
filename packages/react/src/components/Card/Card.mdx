# Card

Displays a card with header, content, and footer.

`CardContent` and `CardFooter` pad their sides and bottom, on SwiftUI and Compose too: a header above them has already paid the
top. With no header, the one that is the card's first child pays the top as well, so content never
touches the card's top edge (GAP-194). A part inside a wrapper, such as a `form` around the card's
parts, is not the card's first child and is left as it is.

`CardTitle` is a heading, and its level follows the page's outline (GAP-205): an `h3` unless `as`
says otherwise. Under a page titled with an `h1`, a card's title is the next level down,
`as="h2"`, or a screen reader's list of headings skips a level. `as` takes `h2` to `h6`, as
`Heading` does; there is no `h1`, the page's own title. The level changes what a screen reader
hears, not how the title looks. On SwiftUI and Compose `KozmosCardTitle` is a heading too, with no
level: VoiceOver's and TalkBack's heading navigation stop on it.

## Header actions

`actions` on `CardHeader` puts what acts on the card at the header's inline end (GAP-169): an edit
IconButton beside the site's name, a menu. The title and description keep the room before it and
their own spacing, and the actions are centred on the title's line, whatever their size, with no
extra height for an icon button at either density. In right to left the actions are at the left. They come after the
title in the reading and tab order. Without `actions`, the header is drawn as it was.

## Subtle header

`tone="subtle"` on `CardHeader` draws it as a pale theme strip (CLOUD-UI-001; decisions 135 and
139): theme 0, full-bleed to the card's edges and inside its top corners, at least 52 tall, its
title 14. It is Pointr Cloud's form side panel header, with the panel's title, its "building /
level" line and its close. Its sides follow the card's `padding`, its block padding is 12, and it
keeps the card's padding below the tint, so the content under it starts where it starts under any
header. With `actions`, the title and the actions are centred in the strip. A `CardTitle` in it is
14 unless its `size` says otherwise. Title, description and a ghost action's ink all read 4.5:1 on
theme 0 in both themes (`tokens:contrast:check`). On SwiftUI it is `KozmosCardHeader(tone:
.subtle)` and on Compose `KozmosCardHeader(tone = KozmosCardHeaderTone.Subtle)`.

## Title size

`size` on `CardTitle` (GAP-035, decision 132): `default` is 24, the title as it always was; `sm`
is 18, for a compact card on a phone or a dashboard's section card; `xs` is 14, the subtle strip's
title. The weight, the tight tracking and the one-line leading stay at every size, and the
heading level stays `as`'s. With `actions`, they are centred on the smaller title's line. SwiftUI
and Compose draw the platform's own steps: `.title3`, `.headline` and `.subheadline` on SwiftUI,
and `titleLarge`, `titleMedium` and `titleSmall` on Compose.
