# TourStep

One step of a product tour (GAP-188, decision 116), drawn as the MAP-64 design draws it (decision
136): a card beside the control the step points at, its arrow pointing at it, which Backdrop's
cut-out lights while the rest of the page is dimmed. It carries the tour's name and its position
("Add users and assign permissions · 1 of 4"), Close, a title, a body, what to do now as a Notice
("Open Settings, then User Management, or select Next to go there."), any further content, the
progress as a bar, and the footer: a dismiss link at the start, then Back, a secondary action and
Next. It is Popover's surface with the Control corner (decision 118), 16 inside, 372 wide, and 432
with the dismiss link.

TourStep is one step, and it is controlled: which step shows, and pausing and resuming the tour,
are the product's. On the docs page each example starts shut; its button opens it. The MAP-64
examples are drawn at compact density, as Pointr Cloud draws its controls (decision 82).

## A tour

Keep one TourStep and change its props as the tour moves on: `step`, `title`, `body`,
`instruction` and `target`. The cut-out glides to the next target, and the card is drawn anew for
each `step`, so a reader hears the new step as a dialog opening, and focus is on its Next. Pass
`onBack` from the second step on, and `nextLabel="Done"` on the last; `onNext` on the last step is
where the tour ends.

## The card, part by part

- **The eyebrow**: `tourLabel` and the position, `"{tour} · {step} of {count}"`, in the theme ink,
  Text xs semibold, in sentence case. `positionLabel` writes the position: a template with `{step}`
  and `{count}` (`"{step} of {count}"` by default) or a function of them.
- **Close**: a ghost icon button at the end of the eyebrow's row, named by `closeLabel` ("Close").
  It is drawn only with `onClose`, which Escape calls too; without it there is no Close, and Escape
  leaves the step open.
- **The title**: a level 2 heading, drawn at Heading 4's size.
- **The body**: `body`, Text sm in the muted ink.
- **The instruction**: what to do now, a Notice that never collapses, its icon centred on its words.
  `instructionTone` is `"info"` (the default, with a pointer) or `"warning"` (with the warning's
  own mark), for a step that can't go on as it should. It sits in a polite live region mounted with
  each step, so an instruction that arrives or changes while the step is open is read out.
- **Content**: `children`, after the instruction, in the card's own ink: Tags, a Table, a link.
- **The progress**: with `stepCount`, a Progress bar and the position beside it ("1 of 4", Text xs
  muted). The bar is named by `progressLabel` ("Tour progress") and says the position as its value.
- **The footer**: `onDismiss` draws a link at the start (`dismissLabel`, "Don't show again"); then,
  at the end, `onBack` draws Back (ghost), `onSecondary` an outline secondary action
  (`secondaryLabel`, "Not now"), and `onNext` Next. Every button is size sm. Each is drawn only
  with its handler.

## A step its own action completes

On a step whose own action moves the tour on (adding a user, drawing a desk), or one waiting for a
valid value, pass `nextDisabled`: Next is drawn disabled, not hidden, so the footer keeps one shape
on every step (MAP-64 D11). The card then takes focus itself.

## The welcome card

With no `stepCount` there is no position and no progress, and with no `onClose` there is no Close.
`onDismiss` draws "Don't show again" at the footer's start; with it, the card is 432 wide rather
than 372, so its three actions sit on one line. The width follows the dismiss link: there is no
width prop.

<Canvas of={TourStepStories.Welcome} />

## Where it sits

`target` is an element, a ref to one, a virtual element (anything with `getBoundingClientRect()`,
such as a point on a map) or a rect in the viewport's coordinates. The card sits on its `side`
(`"bottom"` by default), at `align` along it (`"center"`), its 24 by 12 arrow's tip 3 past the
cut-out's ring, as the design draws it. The arrow covers the card's edge by 1, as every
PopoverArrow does, so the card is 14 past the ring. It flips to the other side where there is
no room, and keeps 16 from the window's edges. It is read every frame, as Backdrop reads its
target, so the card and the cut-out move together, whatever moves the target, and a ref filled
after the step opens is found. To light a whole container round a control, pass the container as
the `target`.

With no target, or one that is not on the page (a ref to nothing, an element with no box), the
whole page is dimmed and the card, with no arrow, places itself in the middle of the window, 420
wide (decision 139): the design's "This step can't be shown", and a tour's ending.

## A single hint (coachmark)

With no `stepCount`, no `onBack` and no `onNext`, TourStep is a coachmark (GAP-157): a title and a
body beside the control it teaches, with no position and no progress, closed by its button and by
Escape. It takes focus on itself when it opens, so it is read out. Name its button with
`closeLabel`, "Dismiss" for example. For an action as well, pass `onNext` with `nextLabel="Got it"`.
There is no separate Coachmark part.

<Canvas of={TourStepStories.Coachmark} />

## Pressing outside, and closing

Escape and Close call `onClose`. A press on the dim or on the lit target does not: the dim and the
target are part of the tour, and the target takes the press through the cut-out, as "Open Settings"
asks. `onInteractOutside` is called on each such press, and when focus moves outside the card; to
close on one, call `onClose` from there. `open={false}` lets the step leave as a popover does; the
product may also stop rendering it.

## Stacking

The card is drawn in the provider's portal, one layer above its Backdrop, whatever the order the
two arrive in, so a backdrop drawn anew never covers the card. Started while a popover is open, its
dim covers the popover and the card is above both; a popover opened while the step is open, from
the card or by the lit target (a field's list, a tooltip), opens above them.

Inside a modal (Dialog, Drawer, BottomSheet), its dim is drawn in the modal's content, where the
modal leaves it live, and the card waits for the modal's content to mount: opened after the
modal's own layer, it is above the modal, takes focus from its trap and is read, as a popover
opened in a modal is. Escape closes the step, not the modal.

## Accessibility

TourStep is a non-modal dialog named by its title and described by its tour, its position, its
body and its instruction, so a reader hears "Open User Management in Settings, dialog, Add users
and assign permissions, 1 of 4, …" when it opens. It takes focus when it opens, on Next, or on
itself when it has no Next or Next is disabled, whether a press opened it or the page did. The page
stays reachable: Tab leaves the card for the lit target, and the target keeps its own role. The
instruction's live region is mounted with each step, and its `aria-live` is written out, so it is
still read while a modal beside the tour hides the rest of the page (GAP-193). The progress bar is
read as the position; the words beside it, and the arrow, are drawn for the eye.

When the step closes with focus lost (Escape, Close, Done), `PopoverContent` gives focus back
(GAP-190): to what had focus as the step opened, such as the button that started the tour, or,
when that is gone or the step opened with focus on the previous step's Next, to the step's target.
A virtual element or a rect has nothing to take it. A step's own Next keeps focus as the next step
opens.

The labels have English defaults (decision 100): `backLabel` "Back", `nextLabel` "Next",
`secondaryLabel` "Not now", `dismissLabel` "Don't show again", `closeLabel` "Close",
`progressLabel` "Tour progress" and `positionLabel` `"{step} of {count}"`.

Right to left, the card, its sections, the progress bar and the footer mirror, as the provider's
direction says. At compact density (Pointr Cloud's), Close and every button are 32; at touch
density, 44. The card arrives without its zoom when motion is reduced, and in forced colours its
arrow is drawn in the system's text colour.

import * as React from "react";
import { Button, TourStep } from "@kozmos-ds/react";

export function SitesTour() {
  const site = React.useRef<HTMLButtonElement>(null);
  const [step, setStep] = React.useState<number | null>(1);
  return (
    <>
      <Button ref={site} variant="outline">
        Healthcare Center
      </Button>
      <TourStep
        open={step !== null}
        target={site}
        tourLabel="How Pointr Cloud is organised"
        step={step ?? 1}
        stepCount={5}
        title="Open a site"
        body="Each site in this client is listed here. Open one to see its buildings."
        instruction="Open a site, or select Next to open the first one."
        onBack={step && step > 1 ? () => setStep(step - 1) : undefined}
        onNext={() => setStep((current) => (current ?? 1) + 1)}
        onClose={() => setStep(null)}
      />
    </>
  );
}
    `}
