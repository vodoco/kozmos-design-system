# PageHeader

The top of a page: where the page is (a breadcrumb), its title with an info button beside it, a line that says what
the page is for, and the page's actions (GAP-036). One PageHeader serves every page (decision 135): Home's band and a
settings page's header are the same part.

  <Story of={PageHeaderStories.Default} />
</Canvas>

<Controls />

## The band

By default the header is a band across the page in the pale theme tint, theme 0, the tint of CardHeader's subtle strip.
Its content sits in a [Container](?path=/docs/foundations-container--docs), so it lines up with the page under it, with
Container's block inset (GAP-116): 32 above and 24 below. `tone="default"` paints no band, for a header on the page's
own background.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={PageHeaderStories.Plain} />
</Canvas>

## Title size

The title is the page's one `h1` at either size. `size="md"`, the default, draws it at Heading 3's 24/32, for a page
within a section; `size="lg"` at Heading 2's 30/36, for a landing page such as Home.

<Canvas>
  <Story of={PageHeaderStories.Landing} />
</Canvas>

## Info and actions

`info` sits on the title's first line, after it: an `IconButton` with its own label, opening a Popover or a Tooltip.
It is not part of the heading, so a screen reader reads the title alone. `actions` sit at the end of the title's line,
centred on it, and wrap under the text where the page is too narrow for both. In right to left they are at the left.
