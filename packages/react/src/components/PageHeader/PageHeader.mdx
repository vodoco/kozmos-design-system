# PageHeader

The top of a page: where the page is (a breadcrumb), its title with an info button beside it, a line that says what
the page is for, and the page's actions (GAP-036). One PageHeader serves every page (decision 135): Home's band and a
settings page's header are the same part.

## The band

By default the header is a band across the page in the pale theme tint, theme 0, the tint of CardHeader's subtle strip.
Its content sits in a [Container](?path=/docs/foundations-container--docs), so it lines up with the page under it, with
Container's block inset (GAP-116): 32 above and 24 below. `tone="default"` paints no band, for a header on the page's
own background.

## Title size

The title is the page's one `h1` at either size. `size="md"`, the default, draws it at Heading 3's 24/32, for a page
within a section; `size="lg"` at Heading 2's 30/36, for a landing page such as Home.

## Info and actions

`info` sits on the title's first line, after it: an `IconButton` with its own label, opening a Popover or a Tooltip.
It is not part of the heading, so a screen reader reads the title alone. `actions` sit at the end of the title's line,
centred on it, and wrap under the text where the page is too narrow for both: the text keeps at least 256 (16rem), so
they stay on the title's line only where 256, the 16 gap and the actions fit, and otherwise sit 16 under the text, at
its start. In right to left they are at the left. On a phone (320 wide, 288 inside) any button wraps.

SwiftUI's and Compose's `KozmosPageHeader` follow the same rule: the actions on the title's line where 256, 16 and the
actions fit, and otherwise 16 under the text at its start (at the right in right to left). An actions slot that draws
nothing, such as an `if` that leaves its button out, takes no room and no gap.

## A title addon

`titleAddon` sits beside the title, after it and before `info`: a node that goes with the title, such as a status
`Tag` ("Changes Awaiting Publish"), as ListItem's `titleAddon` does (decision 210, owner, 17 Nov). It is outside
the `h1`, so the heading is the title alone and a screen reader reads the addon after it, and it is centred on the
title's line, as `info` is. Where the line has no room for the title and the addon, the addon, and the info button
after it, wrap under the title, 8 below it at its start, rather than squeezing the title into a column. With no
addon the title's line is unchanged. SwiftUI takes it as `.titleAddon { … }` on `KozmosPageHeader`, and Compose
as `titleAddon`, a composable, on an overload of `KozmosPageHeader`; both keep the heading on the title alone and
wrap the addon under the title the same way.
