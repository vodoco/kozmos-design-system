# Button

Buttons allow users to take actions, and make choices, with a single tap.

For a full-width action, React uses `className="w-full"`, Compose uses `Modifier.fillMaxWidth()`, and SwiftUI uses `KozmosButton("Done", fillsWidth: true) { ... }`. SwiftUI's opt-in expands the painted label and hit area, not just an outer container; existing buttons keep their intrinsic width by default.

## Emotion

`variant` says how much a button weighs and what shape it takes. `emotion` says what it
means. They are separate axes because the product uses them separately: measured on
2026-09-14 across thirteen product surfaces, it drives six emotions and uses all six.

The token tiers already split the same way — `Components.Primary Buttons` is filled,
`Secondary` is bordered, `Tertiary` is text — and each tier has carried all six emotions
with `idle`, `hover`, `pressed` and `focus` since long before this prop existed. A variant
picks the tier; the emotion picks the colour inside it.

Colours are resolved from the active theme tokens, rather than fixed hex values.

The default, filled and `themed`, is the theme fill: theme 500, the client's base colour set
in the Pointr Cloud Dashboard (`#135BEC`), in both themes, with white text on it in both,
5.62:1 (decision 59). Hovered it is `#1051E8`; focused from the keyboard `#1051E8` inside the
focus ring; and pressed, while the pointer holds it, `#0D44C2`, in both themes. Until
2026-10-07 the pressed and focus tokens were read by no rule, so a pressed button showed its
hover colour and a focused one its idle colour. Every filled emotion, `destructive` and
`secondary` draw their own four states the same way, stepping along their own ramp away from the
page (decision 60): hovered and focused one step, pressed two, darker in the light theme and
lighter in the dark. The dark neutral is the exception and keeps darkening, because white reads
3.72:1 on its lighter step. `secondary` is the neutral fill with its ink on every platform
(decision 61): `#C7CAD1` under black in light, hover and focus `#ABAFBA`, pressed `#9095A2`;
`#464A53` under white in dark, hover and focus `#2E3138`, pressed `#17191C`. Before decision 59
the fill was theme 700: in the dark
theme light blue (`#7EA2F6`) under black text, 8.39:1. Black on the new 500 would read only
3.74:1, which is why the text on it is white in both themes. `outline`, `ghost` and `link`
stay on the `Secondary Buttons` token, theme 700, which turns over with the theme: text on a
surface, not a fill.

Leave `emotion` unset and the variant keeps its own colours: `secondary` draws the `neutral`
tokens and `destructive` the `danger` ones, in every state. Until 2026-10-08 the web's
`secondary` was background/200 with no hover, focus or pressed colour, `#2E3138` at rest in
dark, and SwiftUI's words on it were foreground/100. `glass` is an effect rather than a weight
and takes no emotion.

## Subtle

`variant="subtle"` is a pale theme fill under the theme's words, with a token tier of its own,
`Components.Subtle Buttons` (GAP-200, decision 135): theme 0 under theme 600, the theme as text
(decision 59), 5.72:1 in light and 4.80:1 in dark. Hovered and focused it is theme 100 under
theme 700, and pressed theme 200 under theme 800: the words step with the fill, because theme 600
on theme 100 reads only 4.41:1. Every state is a step of the theme ramp, so a client's ramp
carries it. Pointr Cloud's "+ Add additional field" is one subtle Button, the width of its form.

Set `emotion` and the subtle Button is that emotion's pale fill under its words. Pointr Cloud's
Delete Feature and Delete Beacon are the destructive tone, `emotion="danger"` (decision 139):
danger 0, a rose fill, under the danger text, danger 700. In dark a pressed `alert` keeps its
hover's fill, as no step of the dark alert ramp reads 4.5:1 on its 200.
