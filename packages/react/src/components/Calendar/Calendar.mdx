# Calendar

A month grid to choose a day or a range in, one month or two side by side (decision 104). It is
the panel Figma draws open under DatePicker (`438:4292`) and DateRangePicker (`443:10939`): a
36px day in one month and a 40px day in a range, four apart, with the Control corner; the chosen
day in the theme fill under the theme foreground (decision 59), today on background/100, a
range's days on theme/100, and another month's days in ink 400.

Days are `YYYY-MM-DD` strings, as DatePicker's value is, and a month is `YYYY-MM`. Calendar draws
no surface of its own: DatePicker opens it in a Popover, and a page can set it on a Card.

## A day or a range

`mode="single"` (the default) calls `onValueChange` with the day each time one is chosen, the
chosen one included, so a picker can close on it. `mode="range"` calls it with `{ start }` when
the first day is chosen and `{ start, end }`, in order, when the second is; the next choice starts
a new range. While a range has a start and no end, the day under the pointer or the focus shows
where it would end. `value` with `onValueChange` controls it; `defaultValue` leaves it to the
calendar.

`months={2}` shows two months side by side, 12 apart, and wraps them under each other where they do
not fit. The first month shown is the value's, else today's; `month` with `onMonthChange` controls
it, and `defaultMonth` sets the first. `today` names the day drawn as today, by default the
reader's own: a venue in another time zone can pass its own.

## Days that cannot be chosen

`min` and `max` bound the days: those outside are muted, cannot be chosen, and the keyboard stops at
the bound. `isDateUnavailable` closes any day a product decides, such as a venue's closed days:
those are muted too, and the keyboard still reaches them, so a reader hears that they exist and
that they are unavailable. A range may cross a closed day; only its ends must be open.

## The keyboard

The grid is one tab stop, after the previous- and next-month buttons, on the chosen day, else today,
else the month's first. It follows the WAI-ARIA date picker dialog's grid:

| Key                      | Moves to                                               |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Left and Right arrows    | the day before and after, as the page reads            |
| Up and Down arrows       | the same day a week before and after                   |
| Home and End             | the week's first and last day, from the locale's start |
| Page Up and Page Down    | the same date a month before and after                 |
| Shift+Page Up, Page Down | the same date a year before and after                  |
| Enter and Space          | chooses the day                                        |

Where a month is shorter, the date is its last day. Leaving the months shown shows the next.

## Locale and direction

`locale` names the months and the days and picks the first day of the week; by default it is the
document's language, then the browser's. The weekdays are the locale's short names, and the days
are in its digits. The first day of the week is `Intl.Locale`'s, and CLDR's where an engine has
none (Firefox). Pass `locale` when you render on the server, where there is no document.

Right to left, the week starts at the right, the arrows follow the page and the chevrons turn.

## Accessibility

Each month is a grid named by its title, and the title is a polite live region, so a new month is
read out. Each day is named by its full date in the locale (`Monday, May 25, 2026`) and says
whether it is chosen, today (`aria-current="date"`) or unavailable (`aria-disabled`). Days of
another month are drawn for the eye only. The weekday row is hidden from readers, since every day's
name says its weekday. `previousMonthLabel` and `nextMonthLabel` name the buttons, `"Previous
month"` and `"Next month"` by default (decision 100). `disabled` takes the grid out of the tab order
and turns the buttons off; `readOnly` keeps it reachable and chooses nothing.
