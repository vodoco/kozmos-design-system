# MapControlsGroup

The stacked zoom, compass, and locate affordances that sit over a map.

## Usage

Use it as the standard control cluster on any map surface:

- Inside the `AdaptiveMapShell` controls slot
- Over a `MapView` in a custom layout

Each handler is optional, and omitting one removes its button. A map with fixed
zoom simply does not pass `onZoomIn` / `onZoomOut`, rather than rendering
disabled controls.

`compassBearing` rotates the compass glyph. Pass the renderer's current bearing;
the component does not track it.

Every control wears the SDK's map-control surface — `MapControlButton`'s: a
48px square, no border, and the map controls' three shadows. The zoom pair is
one such surface, its buttons its segments.

`locationState` is a `UserLocationState` — `off`, `locating`, `following`,
`heading`, `permission-denied`, `stale`, or `unavailable`. Several of these are
not errors and should not read as failures: `locating` is in progress, and
`stale` still has a usable last-known fix.

Pair `locationState` with a localized `locationStateLabel`. The visual treatment
alone does not tell a screen reader user whether location is following, denied,
or merely stale.

### The location control's marks

The location control draws a mark for each state, from Pointr's Location
Tracking Buttons revamp:

| State                                   | Mark                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| `off`, `stale`                          | the outline pointer                                       |
| `locating`                              | the outline pointer, with the button's spinner            |
| `following`                             | `LocationFollowing` — the solid pointer and its cone      |
| `heading`                               | `LocationHeading` — the upright pointer and the turn arc  |
| `permission-denied`, `unavailable`      | the pointer struck through                                |

The marks are the SDK's 24px; the two symbols come from `@kozmos-ds/icons` and
their cone and arc reach past that square, as the revamp's do. They take the
control's colour: grey while the map is not following, the theme's blue while
it follows or turns. To draw your own mark for a state, pass that state in
`locationIcons`; the others keep the group's marks.

### The SDK's location control

The control reads as the SDK's Tracking Indicator (Figma
`ce7phRJR1sCkH6zT8EMH8I`, `434:31572`; decision 40):

- **"Focus" over "Off" or "On"**, two equal lines, bold 16px on a 16px line.
  Off, the words and the mark are grey (foreground/400); on, the words are navy
  (theme/1000) and the mark the theme's blue (theme/600). No border marks "On":
  the words and the mark carry it.
- **Heading reads "On"**, as following does. Its mark tells the two apart on
  screen; a screen reader hears `locationHeadingDescription` after the words —
  "Focus, On, map turns with you" — which the product translates.
- **No position reads one line**: with `unavailable` or `permission-denied` the
  control draws `locationStateLabel` alone — the SDK's "No Location" — and its
  name still starts what a screen reader hears ("Focus, No Location").
- `locating`, `stale` and `permission-denied` keep their meaning in the same
  style: the spinner beside the outline for "Focus / Locating", the outline for
  "Focus / Last known", the struck pointer for a state the product names.

It is icon-only over the map and widens to say its new mode for a moment when
the mode changes. Ask for that with `locationRevealOnChange` and
`locationLabelPlacement="stacked"`. Both are off by default, so a product that
relies on a fixed presentation sees nothing move. From following to heading the
words stay "On", so nothing widens; the SDK says "Map Rotation: On" in a status
message of its own, and a product that wants the same can show a `Toast`.

### Step-free during wayfinding

While a route is shown, a step-free control takes the location control's place.
Passing `onStepFreeChange` draws it, and the location control is not drawn; leave
it out once the route is gone and the location control returns. It reads as the
location control does — it follows `locationPresentation`,
`locationRevealOnChange` and `locationLabelPlacement` — so the corner keeps its
character between exploring and wayfinding.

Set `stepFree` once the route it describes is the one on the map. The name and
its two states are `stepFreeLabel`, `stepFreeOnLabel` and `stepFreeOffLabel`
("Step-free", "On", "Off"), for the product to translate.

## Accessibility

- Every control is the SDK's 48px square, above the shared 44px touch target,
  including in the icon-only presentation; nothing lets it shrink below 44px.
- `label` names the cluster so it is announced as one group of controls rather
  than a run of unlabelled buttons.
- `locationState` must be conveyed through `locationStateLabel`, not by the icon
  treatment alone. The marks and the tones are decorative: the button's name
  carries the state. Heading shows following's "On", so its name goes on with
  `locationHeadingDescription`, after the words shown, so the name still begins
  with what a voice-control user sees.
- No border draws the control's edge; a keyboard focus ring does, in the ring's
  colour (theme/600, over 6:1 against the surface in both themes). The zoom
  pair's buttons draw theirs inside themselves, where the pair cannot cut them.
- The step-free control is a toggle: it reports `aria-pressed`, and its name
  carries its state ("Step-free, On"), as the location control's does.
- The compass glyph does not auto-mirror in RTL; a bearing is physical.
