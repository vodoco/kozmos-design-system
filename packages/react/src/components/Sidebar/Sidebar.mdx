# Sidebar

Vertical navigation shell. Compose persistent destinations with `NavigationItem` inside the `navigation` slot, and place workspace, tools, or account surfaces through the named `header`, `tools`, and `footer` slots.

## Rail

`variant="rail"` (or `collapsed`) is Kozmos's rail: **96px** on the surface, with a **1px edge** in the
border role at its inline end, the right, and the left right to left. It has no horizontal padding,
so the `NavigationItem` rail items in its `navigation` fill it; stack them in a column with no gap
between them. Its header and footer stay centred. See NavigationItem's Rail section for the items.

<Canvas of={SidebarStories.Rail} />

## Flush under a header

`inset="flush"` starts the sidebar's first slot at its top edge (GAP-199): a rail under a fixed
header, its first item flush under it, as Pointr Cloud draws its side menu. The default
pads the top 24, and the navigation 16 more, so the first item sits 40 below the edge. On a rail
whose navigation is first, flush drops both: rail items ring their focus inside themselves, so the
first one's ring is whole at the edge. With a `header`, the header starts at the edge and the
navigation keeps its 16 below it. An expanded sidebar drops the 24 and keeps the 16, the room its
items' rings take outside them, which the scrolling navigation would otherwise cut. The bottom keeps
its room either way.
