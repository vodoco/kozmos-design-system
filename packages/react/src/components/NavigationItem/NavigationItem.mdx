# NavigationItem

Reusable navigation row/item primitive for top navigation, side navigation, and the rail.

<Story of={NavigationItemStories.Side} />

## Rail

A rail is a column of rail items, drawn as the dashboard side menu is. An item **fills the width of
its rail**, and the rail is **96px**, its 1px edge included. The item grows with its label: **16px
by 8px of padding**, a **24px icon 6px above an 11px regular label on 14px lines**, up to two lines,
so it is 76px tall, or 90px with two lines. A label that needs a third line is cut at the end of the
second.

At rest an item is the muted foreground, with no fill. Selected, it is primary on the lightest theme
tint (theme/0), with a **2px primary bar down its inline end**: the right, and the left right to
left. The item is square, so the bar runs its whole height, and it draws its focus ring inside
itself, where a rail that scrolls cannot clip it.

`density` sizes top and side items. A rail item has one size, and draws the same with `compact`:
the fixed 72px and 64px tiles are retired.

<Canvas of={NavigationItemStories.Rail} />

### The rail itself

Kozmos's rail is Sidebar's `rail` variant: 96px on the surface, with a 1px edge in the border role at
its inline end and no horizontal padding, so its items fill it. A rail of your own needs the same: 96px
wide, its items in a column with no gap between them.

<Canvas of={NavigationItemStories.RailRightToLeft} />

### Long labels

These nine labels, a web dashboard's, are the longest a rail has been asked to hold. In the 96px rail
each takes at most two lines and no word splits across them, measured in Chromium, Firefox and
WebKit, in narrow and wide fonts. A 72px tile left its label 56px, and "Configuration" is 73px at
11px, which is why the rail is 96px.

<Canvas of={NavigationItemStories.DashboardRail} />

The native rails are the same: iOS sets the label in `caption2` (11pt at the default text size) on
14pt lines and Android in `labelSmall` (11sp) on 14sp lines, both growing with the reader's text
size, and `KozmosSidebar`'s rail is 96 wide on both.
