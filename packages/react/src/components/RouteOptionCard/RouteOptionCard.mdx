# RouteOptionCard

Controlled route alternative with localized duration and distance labels,
selection semantics, availability, and warning support.

An option is a card of its own: the background colour, and the chosen one
the theme's 5% tint on it. The tint lay over nothing, so on a glass sheet the
map showed through the chosen option while the others stood opaque; it now
reads like them, and on a solid sheet it looks as it did. The same on iOS and
Android.
