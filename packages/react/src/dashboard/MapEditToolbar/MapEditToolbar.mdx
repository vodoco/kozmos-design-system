# MapEditToolbar

Pointr Cloud's map edit toolbar (CLOUD-UI-005): one part for its seven geometry toolbars, a card on the map of tools, actions and dividers, with one tool chosen at a time. A Dashboard module: it ships from `@kozmos-ds/react/dashboard`, with `@kozmos-ds/react/dashboard.css` loaded after the package's `style.css`, and only on the web.

The card is 60 tall, with padding 6 and 8, a corner of 16 and the map controls' lift (decisions 139 and 151). Each item is 56 wide and 48 tall, a 24 icon over an 11/14 label. A tool is a ToggleButton, pressed while it is chosen; an action is a ghost Button. The product decides which items each mode shows, and passes them as data: the toolbar draws them and reports what is pressed.

## One tab stop

The toolbar is one tab stop (`role="toolbar"`, named by `label`): Tab reaches the chosen tool, or the first item, and leaves. The arrow keys move between the items in the writing direction, wrapping at the ends, and Home and End go to the first and the last. A disabled item is skipped, as every disabled Kozmos button is. Every item is named by its label; a tool says whether it is pressed (`aria-pressed`). A `tooltip` describes an item where it adds to the label, on hover and on keyboard focus.

## How each state is drawn

The items are drawn as Pointr Cloud's live toolbar draws them (decision 226). No item has a fill at rest.

- **Enabled**, a tool not chosen or an action: the label in the page's ink (foreground 0: black in light, white in dark), the icon in Cloud's grey (foreground 400).
- **Chosen**: label and icon in the theme's ink on the surface (the themed emotion's text, theme 600), with no fill, not ToggleButton's pressed theme fill. The focus ring is kept.
- **Hovered**, enabled or chosen: the hover fill (decision 173), the inks kept.
- **Disabled**: label and icon in foreground 600, at full strength, with no fill: 3.0:1 on the white card (2.997:1) and 3.90:1 on the black one. The toolbar is an exception to decision 197's disabled fill, which every other Kozmos button draws: Cloud shows an unavailable tool as lighter words with no box.

## Saying why an item is unavailable

A disabled item can say why with `disabledReason` (decision 217): MAP-619's Merge waits for two areas to be selected. With a reason, the item is not removed from the keyboard's way: it stays in the arrow-key order and under the pointer, says it is unavailable with `aria-disabled="true"` rather than `disabled`, and does nothing when it is clicked or pressed with Enter or Space, so neither `onValueChange` nor `onAction` hears of it. Its reason is its tooltip, on hover and on keyboard focus, and its accessible description, in place of any `tooltip`. It is drawn as any disabled item (below), under the pointer too. Without a reason, a disabled item is skipped and shows nothing, as before. In the More menu a disabled item is a disabled entry, reason or not.

```tsx
{
  type: "tool",
  id: "merge",
  label: "Merge",
  icon: Divider,
  disabled: true,
  disabledReason: "Select two areas to merge them",
}
```

## On a narrow map

The toolbar spans the space it is given and centres its card in it. When the items do not fit, those that do not go, from the end, into a More menu: a tool there is a checkbox item, checked while chosen, and an action a menu item. Nothing runs off the screen.
