# Changelog: Kozmos for Android (Jetpack Compose)

Kozmos is consumed from this repository's `packages/android` source as an Android library module in the `com.kozmos` namespace. It is not a published Maven/registry release. Pin a reviewed repository commit when adopting these changes; the version headings below follow the web release plan, not a separate native package publication.

## Unreleased — repository source

Native source changes on `main` after the 0.6.0 snapshot below. Nothing here is tagged.

### Fixed

- **A toast laid out in place tells TalkBack it has appeared:** `KozmosToast(title = …)` and `KozmosToast(content = …)` were no live region, so TalkBack said nothing when one appeared. React's toast is Radix's, which announces the toast's words through an assertive live region for the default foreground toast. The toast is now an assertive live region (`LiveRegionMode.Assertive`), and TalkBack says its words as it appears. `KozmosToast(hostState = …)` is unchanged: Material's `SnackbarHost` already makes each toast a polite live region, and the toast adds none of its own there, so TalkBack says it once. The look and the signatures are unchanged.
- **The backdrop is no control TalkBack or a keyboard stops on:** `KozmosBackdrop` took its tap through `clickable`, so TalkBack focused it as a control with no name or role ("Double tap to activate") that dismissed whatever it dimmed, and a hardware keyboard's Tab stopped on it. React's Backdrop is a plain `div`, with no role, name or tab stop, and SwiftUI's tap gesture on the scrim is no accessibility element. The scrim now takes a finger's tap through a tap gesture: a tap still calls `onTap` once and reaches nothing under the scrim, but the scrim has no role, action or focus, and what it dims is read as before. Dismissing for TalkBack and the keyboard is the enclosing overlay's, as the docs say: its close button and Back. The look and the signature are unchanged.
- **The colour picker's format row grows with large text and wraps as React's does:** the open `KozmosColorPicker`'s row of the format menu, the value boxes, the opacity box and its "%" was a Row of boxes a fixed 36dp tall, which could not wrap. From font scale 1.3 every box cut its text top and bottom, at every width (at 1.3, 25.5dp of text in a 23.6dp text box; at 2, 34.9dp). Where the row ran out of room it squeezed its last parts: at font scale 2 the "%" was drawn 0dp wide at 320 and 343dp, and at 320dp the opacity value broke over three lines and HEX's value over two. A box is now at least 36dp and grows with its text. Where the parts do not fit on one line at their text's width, the row wraps as React's format grid does, `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(3.5rem, 1fr))`: equal columns, at least 56dp (96 for HEX) and at least as wide as the widest part, as many to a line as fit, in reading order from the inline start, mirrored right to left; the opacity box and its "%" share a column. At font scale 2 on a 320dp picker that is the menu, hue and saturation on the first line and lightness and opacity on the second, in three 94.5dp columns; HEX gets two. Where the row fitted, as it does at every width from 320 to 430dp at font scale 1, it is laid out exactly as before, in both directions and both themes, and TalkBack is given the same words in the same order.
- **The colour picker's square keeps its handle on its colour right to left:** `KozmosColorPicker`'s saturation and lightness square is drawn left to right in either reading direction, white at the left and the hue at the right, as React draws it, but its handle was placed with `TopStart` and `offset`, which mirror right to left. So right to left the handle stood at the wrong end: a fully saturated colour's handle sat over the white. The handle is placed from the left, with `AbsoluteAlignment.TopLeft` and `absoluteOffset`, so it sits over its colour in both directions. Left to right it draws exactly as before, and the swatch, the open button and the sliders still follow the reading direction.
- **The colour picker's palette field grows with large text:** the open `KozmosColorPicker`'s palette field, under the format row, was a Row a fixed 40dp tall, its label free to wrap and laid out before the caret. At font scale 1.5 on a 320dp picker, and at 2 up to 359dp, the label broke over two lines and the fixed height cut the second off: at 2 and 320dp the field showed "Kozmos Design" of "Kozmos Design System 2.0". There the caret was squeezed to 0dp, and at 2 and 390dp to 17dp, against the label. From font scale 1.3 the field did not grow at all. It is now at least 40dp and 8dp taller than its label above and below (50.9dp at font scale 2), as React's 2.25rem `<select>` grows with its text. The label stays on one line, as React's and SwiftUI's do, and ends in an ellipsis where it does not fit beside the caret (at 1.5 up to 343dp, at 2 up to 390dp); TalkBack still reads it whole. The caret keeps its 20dp at the inline end with at least 8dp before it, as SwiftUI's `Spacer(minLength: 8)` keeps. At font scale 1, at every width from 320 to 430dp, in both directions and both themes, the field, its label and its caret are laid out exactly as before.
- **A Tag's remove button is named in the product's words:** `KozmosTag` takes a trailing `removeLabel` ("Remove" by default, as React's), and its X is a button (`Role.Button`) that TalkBack reads by that name; it was an image with a fixed "Remove". With an `emotion`, the X is drawn in the emotion's ink, as the words are: it took the variant's, a white X on a destructive Tag's light green success surface. Its 48dp touch target and the Tag's size are unchanged.
- **A CJK category name gets three lines (decision 78, GAP-010):** `KozmosCategoryTile` gives a name in Han, kana or Hangul a third line, so a long word such as カスタマーサービス shows whole rather than cut; other scripts keep two.
- **The shell's panel keeps 16 below its content, and scrolls to its edge (GAP-085):** `KozmosAdaptiveMapShell`'s panel padded nothing below what it hosts, so a short result list ended against a side panel's bottom edge, and in a sheet against the navigation bar's inset. As on iOS and the web, content that does not scroll now ends 16dp above a side panel's bottom edge, and 16dp above the safe-drawing inset in a sheet (the inset + 16), as it starts 16 below the top. Scrolling content runs to the panel's edge, in a sheet under the navigation bar, and only scrolled to its end does its last row stop 16 above the edge (16 above the bar in a sheet). The new `LocalKozmosPanelInsetBottom` says what the panel keeps below its content, 16 in a shell and 0 outside one, as `LocalKozmosPanelInsetTop` does for the top, and a part with its own end padding and no surface of its own tops it up rather than adding to it: `KozmosRoutePreviewPanel`, whose footer does not scroll, still ends 16 above the edge, not 32. A bordered part keeps its padding inside its border.
  - **A scroller in the panel:** the new `kozmosPanelScrollEndPadding(): Dp` is the end content padding for the scroller that ends the panel: 16dp in a side panel, the safe-drawing bottom inset + 16 in a sheet. Calling it claims the panel's end, so the shell stops padding below the content and the scroller runs to the edge. Put it inside the scroll: `LazyColumn(contentPadding = PaddingValues(bottom = kozmosPanelScrollEndPadding()))`, or `Modifier.verticalScroll(state).padding(bottom = kozmosPanelScrollEndPadding())`, as `docsnippets/PanelScrollingResults.kt` compiles it. Call it once, in ordinary composition (not inside a lazy item or `BoxWithConstraints`), for the scroller that is the panel's last thing; it returns 0, and claims nothing, outside a shell or where `LocalKozmosPanelInsetBottom` is 0. `KozmosBrowseCategoriesPanel`'s grid and `KozmosPOIDetailPanel`'s body in its sheet presentation claim it themselves. `KozmosPOIResultList` does not scroll: give its scroller the padding.
  - **The sheet's content height:** at the largest detent the sheet measured its content as tall as the sheet, not counting the handle's 16dp row, so content that filled it ran 16 past the sheet's edge. It now ends at the edge.
  - Migration: a side panel or a sheet fitted to its content is 16dp taller. A scroller of your own that ends the panel stops 16 above the edge until it reads `kozmosPanelScrollEndPadding()` as its end padding, as above; a part of your own that pads its own end and does not scroll can read `LocalKozmosPanelInsetBottom` and top up to it. Review screenshot baselines with a shell panel.
- **Tabs say which tab is selected:** each `KozmosTabsTrigger` is a tab (`Role.Tab`) that TalkBack reads as selected or not selected, as React's `tab` with `aria-selected` (WCAG 4.1.2). `KozmosTabsList` is a selectable group, so TalkBack is given the tabs as one collection, as React's `tablist`. Before, a trigger was a plain clickable with no role or state, and the selected tab was shown only by its segment. TalkBack still presses the other tabs; as for any selected node in Compose, it offers no press on the selected one. The look, the ripple, the signatures and the 44dp target are unchanged.
- **Result rows are heard once:** TalkBack read a `KozmosPOIResultCard` row's whole description and then every text drawn inside it again (name, category, level, availability, walk, the language disclosure and the unavailable reason), and with a `selectionLabel` it read the drawn texts after the product's label. The drawn texts are now left out of semantics, so the row says its words, or the product's label, once. The row keeps its click, enabled state and selection, and a logo keeps its alt text.
- **Map credits are never clipped (GAP-135, decision 58):** `KozmosAdaptiveMapShell` no longer caps the attribution's height and scrolls it when an opened top-bar card and the sheet leave it too little room. The Pointr logo gives way first: `KozmosMapAttribution` leaves out its brand and the credits keep their full height. The panel's sizing still reserves the whole attribution (at most half the band below the top bar), so the logo giving way never moves the sheet. The room counts the lift above the bottom corners when they leave no 128dp middle slot. Credits still too tall to sit above the corners send the corners away (not placed, with no semantics, as when they do not fit), and the credits return to the bottom row. The logo comes back once it fits again: while it has given way, the shell measures an unplaced copy of the whole attribution.
- **Progress reads against its track:** `KozmosProgress` draws React's colours, `primitivesColorsTheme600` on `primitivesColorsBackground200`. Theme 500 on background 300 read 2.56:1 in light and 1.58:1 in dark, under the 3:1 a bar needs against its track.
- **Focus shows on filled controls:** a focused filled `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton`, `KozmosFloatingActionButton`, `KozmosSplitButton` half or filled `KozmosMapControlButton` draws React's ring: 2dp of theme 600, 2dp outside its shape, with the page colour in the gap (6.24:1 on the light page, 6.17:1 on the dark; the gap 5.62:1 and 3.74:1 on the fill). The focus colour alone was 1.11:1 from the fill.
- **Quick taps show:** a tap in scrolling content, where the press is delayed and released at once, is drawn pressed for at least 150 ms; a cancelled press ends at once.
- **Map control states:** a disabled or loading `KozmosMapControlButton` is drawn at 50%, its shadow included; one turned on from the keyboard keeps its focus colour; and a disabled or loading fill ignores presses a shared interaction source reports.
- **Material's colours are Kozmos's:** `KozmosThemeProvider` gave Material its stock `lightColorScheme()` and `darkColorScheme()`, so whatever a part left to Material drew Material's baseline: purple text-selection handles and highlights in every text field, a purple focus border and label on `KozmosDatePicker`'s and `KozmosTimePicker`'s fields, a purple-tinted surface under `KozmosMenu`, `KozmosSplitButton`'s menu, `KozmosSelect`, `KozmosBottomSheet` and `KozmosDrawer`'s sheet (the drawer's was `#F7F2F9` in light and `#25232A` in dark), and `#1C1B1F` / `#E6E1E5` words wherever a part reads `onSurface`: the labels of `KozmosCheckbox`, `KozmosSwitch`, `KozmosRadio`, `KozmosInput`, `KozmosCombobox` and the Listbox's options among them. Every Material colour role is now a Kozmos token in both themes, React's role where React has one: `primary` theme 600 with foreground/1000 on it; `background` and `surface` background/0, with `surfaceTint` the surface itself, so a lifted surface is never tinted; `onBackground` and `onSurface` foreground/100; `surfaceVariant` surface/200 under foreground/400; `outline` the input border and `outlineVariant` the subtle border; `error` danger 600 with foreground/1000 on it, and the danger emotion's surface and ink for its container; `inversePrimary` theme 400 on foreground/100; `secondary` and `tertiary` theme 700 and 800, their containers theme 100 and 200 under 900 and 1000, so no role is purple; `scrim` Kozmos's scrim. Each pair reads at 4.5:1 or more in both themes. Selected text is highlighted in theme 600 at 40% under theme 600 handles: the words on it read 9.01:1 in light and 9.52:1 in dark.

### Added and changed

- **Code Connect maps the library icons:** every `Icon / *` component on the Icons page, the 80 React maps, reads in Dev Mode as `KozmosIcon(name = "<name>")` with its import, a Pointr Maps - Express wayfinding glyph by its name too. An icon nested in a set read back as React's `<Icon name=…/>`, because Compose mapped none of them. `com/kozmos/components/Icon/IconLibrary.figma.kt` is generated with React's by `pnpm figma:iconconnect`; nothing a product calls changes.
- **LocationPin draws the SDK's map result markers (decision 71, rulings 79–81, GAP-120):** in production every one of these markers comes from the map style's sprite sheet, and a featured tile with its logo comes from a featured sprite that Pointr's back end generates. `KozmosLocationPin` draws the same markers for prototypes and boards. It draws one of three, in `KozmosThemeTokens` colours in both themes:
  - **Result** (the default): a pill 32dp tall, at least 32 wide and 8 wider than its `number` on each side, with a 2dp border, and a 12×7 pointer whose tip, 6 below the pill, is the anchor. At rest it is background/0, with the border, pointer and number in theme 600. `selected` fills it with theme 600 and turns the number foreground/1000, and it does not grow. Without a number, an 8dp dot takes the number's place. The number is 14 semibold with tabular figures, at a fixed size as the sprite draws it.
  - **Featured** (`featured`): a tile 44dp (52 when `selected`), radius 16, background/0 in a 3dp foreground/0 rim, with `markerContent`, the place's logo, fitted inside the rim and clipped to radius 13. Below it is a 6dp foreground/0 dot, its top 4 below the tile; the dot's centre is the anchor. It has no number, no star and no accent colour.
  - **Place** (the new `place: Boolean = false`): a place on its own with its details open. It is a teardrop on a 40dp circle in `primitivesColorsEmotionalSuccess800`, with a 20dp background/0 disc and a 6dp dot of the same green, its top 6 below the circle; the dot's centre is the anchor. It wins over `featured` and `number`, and `selected` changes nothing.
  - The body (pill and pointer, tile, teardrop) casts the md shadow, `KozmosShadows.semanticsElevationFloating`; the dots cast none. Before Android 10 an outline that is not convex casts no shadow, so there the pill casts it without its pointer.
  - **Anchoring:** the new `Modifier.kozmosLocationPinAt { IntOffset }` puts the anchor on a map's screen point, in pixels from the top left of a `Box` that fills the map, in either layout direction. The point is read at placement, so a moving map re-places its pins without composing them again. `KozmosLocationPinAnchorX` and `KozmosLocationPinAnchorY` are the alignment lines a renderer's own `Layout` can read. `docsnippets/ResultMarkersOnAMap.kt` compiles the pattern.
  - **Taps:** the new `onClick: (() -> Unit)? = null` is the marker's tap. The new `onOpen: (() -> Unit)? = null` opens the place's details on a second tap within the double-tap time, gated as `KozmosPOIResultCard`'s `onOpen` is: the first tap clicks at once, the second opens once and does not click again, and a quick third does nothing. With `onOpen`, TalkBack is offered opening as a custom action named by the new `openLabel: String = "Open details"`.
  - **Target and semantics:** the target is at least 48dp each way, centred on the body, and never moves the anchor. The marker draws no ripple. It is a button when it can be pressed and an image otherwise. `label` is its whole name, as the product composes it with the number included ("Result 3, Burger King"); Kozmos adds only `featuredLabel` on a featured tile. It reads as selected when `selected`, and a place always does. `enabled = false` draws the whole marker at 50% and takes no taps: a press or a pan that starts on it reaches the map beneath, and with an action TalkBack still hears a disabled button.
  - **Analytics:** a tap sends `location_pin_clicked` (with `kind`, `number` and `selected`) and an open `location_pin_opened` (with `kind` and `number`) through `LocalKozmosAnalytics`, as React and SwiftUI send them.
  - **`externalLabel: String? = null`** is the words drawn beside the marker at `labelPlacement`, as React's `externalLabel` is. They are not read; TalkBack hears `label`. `Left` and `Right` are the map's, physical in every language, as MapLibre places a symbol's text.
  - This replaces what the entries below say of the pin's look: decision 55's quiet pin, decision 59's theme-500 fill, decision 63's accent pin, and decision 68's amber featured pin with its star.
- **A double tap opens a result (decision 72, GAP-143):** `KozmosPOIResultCard`, `KozmosPOIResultList` and `KozmosPOIResultGroup` take `onOpen: ((String) -> Unit)? = null`, the list and the group giving it to every card. The first tap on a result selects it at once, as before; a second tap on the same result within the double-tap time (`ViewConfiguration`'s, 300 ms) calls `onOpen(poiId)` once and is not passed on as a second `onSelect`, so the first tap's selection stands; a third quick tap does nothing. Selected or not, a group's member included, as React's. Only an available result opens. TalkBack's double tap is one activation, a selection, so with `onOpen` the row offers opening as a custom action named by the new `openLabel: String = "Open details"`; without `onOpen` there is no such action and every tap selects, as before. An opened result reports `poi_result_opened` to analytics. Both parameters sit before `onAction`, which stays last for a trailing lambda, and an overload of each part keeps 0.10.0's positional call.
  - Migration: pass a translated `openLabel` with `onOpen`.
- **ManoeuvreCard's grip is near its edge and quieter, and the card is shorter (decision 74, GAP-140):** under its content (the instruction row closed, the itinerary open) `KozmosManoeuvreCard` keeps 12dp, the 36×5 grip and 4dp to its bottom edge: 21dp in all, where a 48dp row with the grip centred in it, about 25 above the edge, took 64. The grab bar is laid out as tall as the grip, so the closed card is 43dp shorter. Its touch target is still 48dp tall, reaching up from the card's bottom edge and never down onto the map. Closed, it reaches over the grip, the gap and the bottom 27dp of the instruction row, and a touch there finds the bar first (the row does the same). Open, a fixed 27dp sits between the itinerary's scroller and the gap, so the scroller ends 48dp above the card's edge and the target covers none of the itinerary's rows at any scroll position; the open card is 16dp shorter. On the theme appearance the bar is the guidance foreground at 62%, quieter than the instruction and 3.09:1 on the theme fill (white was 5.62:1, and 60% would read 2.99:1); on the background appearance it keeps background/300. What TalkBack hears, its names, its actions and focus are unchanged.
  - Migration: review screenshot baselines and any layout that assumes the card's height.
- **Featured is the accent (decisions 67, 68):**
  - `KozmosPOIResultCard`'s Featured tab and a featured card's edge draw `semanticsAccentFill` / `semanticsAccentOnfill`. That is `#FAB735` under black by default, in both appearances and both presentations. They drew the alert fill pair (`#F9A707` light, `#FBC459` dark).
  - A featured `KozmosLocationPin` is filled with `semanticsAccentFill` (it was `primitivesColorsEmotionalAlert500`) and never shows its number. It shows the new `markerContent`, the place's logo, or without one a filled star as tall as the number's digits, in `semanticsAccentOnfill`. Off the floor, its dashed ring is the accent and its star is the foreground.
  - `markerContent: (@Composable () -> Unit)? = null`, a trailing lambda, shows in place of the number on any pin, as React's does. It takes the number's colour and font, is clipped to the disc inside the ring, is not read by TalkBack, and keeps the pin filled.
  - `featuredLabel: String = "Featured"` replaces the hard-coded "Featured" in what TalkBack says. A pin showing a logo or a star no longer says its number.
  - Both parameters come last, with defaults, so existing calls compile.
  - Migration: review screenshots with featured pins (now a star) and featured cards. MapStatusPill's Turn Back stays on the alert colours.
- **Secondary button (decision 61):** no change. `KozmosButton` and `KozmosIconButton` `Secondary` already draw `KozmosFillStates.neutral`, fill and ink, in every state, and React and SwiftUI now match them.
- **Emotion states step away from the page (decision 60):** a filled emotion part's hover and focus are one step, and its pressed two steps, further along the emotion's own ramp: darker in light, lighter in dark. This covers `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton` and every part drawn through `FillStates.kt`.
  - Danger in light is `#B01736`, then `#8C132B`, then `#670E20` (the hover was `#D41C42`, lighter than the fill).
  - In dark, danger, success, alert and informative step lighter, 700 → 800 → 900. Light neutral presses to `#9095A2`, where it went lighter (`#E3E4E8`).
  - Dark neutral keeps darkening, `#464A53` → `#2E3138` → `#17191C`, because white reads 3.72:1 on its lighter step.
  - The colours come from `KozmosThemeTokens`, which `pnpm tokens:native:copy` regenerated.
- **Brand variant 1's 500 is `#4135F1` (decision 63):** `primitivesColorsThemeVariant1500`, the accent `KozmosLocationPin`'s fill, is lightened from `#4134F1` just enough to read 3:1 on the dark page (3.00:1). White on it reads 6.99:1.
- **Tabs draw React's raised segment (decision 65):**
  - `KozmosTabsList` is a background/100 track with the control radius.
  - The selected `KozmosTabsTrigger` is a background/0 segment 4dp inside it, its corner concentric, raised by `KozmosShadows.semanticsElevationRaised`, with foreground/0 words.
  - The other tabs' words are foreground/400. All tabs use `labelLarge` (14sp medium) on one line, ellipsised.
  - Before, the list was background/0 with a 2dp theme-500 bar under the selected tab's foreground/100 words; the other tabs were foreground/500. Nothing on tabs is the theme's colour now.
  - Each tab's target is the track's whole 44dp height, and the press and focus ripple is drawn in the segment's shape. The signatures are unchanged.
  - Migration: review screenshot baselines for screens with tabs. A long label now truncates on one line.
- **Result summaries and their language:** `KozmosPOIResultPresentation.summaryLanguage` names the language of `summary` when it differs from the interface's (GAP-125). `KozmosPOIResultCard` now draws `summary`, muted and two lines at most, after the location, as the web card does, and TalkBack hears it after the location, with a `LocaleSpan` when `summaryLanguage` is set, as `nameLanguage` gives the name one. The name and the summary take their direction from their own words (`TextDirection.Content`), so an Arabic summary in an English card runs right to left; their lines still start at the card's start.
- **A result row's words are its text:** `KozmosPOIResultCard` gives TalkBack the row's words as its semantics text, never as a content description, whether or not a phrase carries a language (GAP-125). The words are the same; a tagged phrase keeps its `LocaleSpan`.
- **POI details model:** `KozmosPOIDetailPanel` takes `details: KozmosPOIDetailsPresentation`, as SwiftUI and React do. It adds the travel estimate on Go and the supplementary actions (book, call) after the POI's own. Under the strip come the summary row (at most three facts), then the attribute groups, the opening hours, the description with Read more, and the tags. The new parameters follow the released ones.
- **Summary tones:** a fact's value and icon use the emotion's text role for success, warning and danger (`semanticsEmotionSuccessText`, `semanticsEmotionAlertText`, `semanticsEmotionDangerText`) and `primitivesColorsTheme600` for brand, as the web's summary names them. Each reads at 4.5:1 or more on the card's white and the sheet's grey in both themes; the fill colours first copied from SwiftUI read as low as 1.57:1.
- **Summary marks:** rating, accessibility, dietary and crowd facts draw Pointr's Star01, the accessibility mark, Feather and ClockPlus, as React does. None mirrors in right to left.
- **Icons:** `KozmosIcon` and the details chips draw every name the web registry knows. The 18 Compose lacked draw Pointr's own outlines, generated from `packages/icons` by `pnpm icons:native:generate` (once `icons:compose:generate`; it writes SwiftUI's too): arrow-down, arrow-up, bookmark, calendar-check-01, clock-plus, eye, feather, flip-backward, globe-02, heart, layout-alt-02, loading-01, mail-01, phone, share-01, shopping-bag-02, stars-01 and switch-vertical-01.
- **Container inset (GAP-134):** a `KozmosContainer(inset: KozmosContainerInset, modifier, content)` overload, as React's `inset`. `Window` pads the sides 16, 24 or 32dp with the width it is given (from 640 and from 1024dp), for a page; `Panel` keeps 16 at any width, for a sheet or side panel. It fills the width, pads the sides only, and answers intrinsic measurements. `KozmosContainer(modifier, content)` is unchanged: it still pads all four sides by 16.
- **Empty state progress (GAP-115):** `KozmosEmptyState` takes `progress: KozmosEmptyStateProgress(value, label, valueText)`, after `modifier`: a long wait it explains, such as a download. It is drawn under the description and above the action, at most 280dp wide: `label` above the bar, and `valueText` ("12 of 30 MB") beside it when given. `value` runs from 0 to 1; outside that it is clamped, and NaN is 0. TalkBack hears one node: the label, then `valueText` or the rounded percentage as its state, with the bar's range.
- **RouteSummary actions and route preview (GAP-110, GAP-111, decision 57):** the navigation layout's `KozmosRouteSummary` gains `locationText` and `actions`, before `progress`, and its `onEndRoute` is now `(() -> Unit)? = null`.
  - `actions` are the journey's: Previous and Next in static wayfinding, Go and Details in the route preview. They are laid out in equal columns in reading order, each as tall as the tallest, and a `KozmosButton` there fills its column, its label wrapping, its 48dp touch target kept. The host owns what they do, when they are disabled, and announcing the new step. A trailing lambda is still `progress`.
  - Without `onEndRoute` there is no End: the route preview, where `locationText` is the place's muted line under the destination.
  - The estimate layout takes `endRouteLabel` (End's content description) and `startNavigationLabel` (Start's label), as React's props of the same names, before `transportModeIcon`. They default to the words drawn before, "End route" and "Start Navigation". Its End icon button now takes Material's own 48dp box; a fixed 40dp size had left the rest to touch-only widening.
- **Itinerary Change actions (GAP-104):** `KozmosItinerary` takes `onEditOrigin` and `onEditDestination`. Each ends its endpoint's row in a ghost Change button, drawn only when passed, at the row's end on the name's first baseline, and offered at most half the row so a long verb wraps; the host opens the endpoint for editing and moves focus there. `changeLabel` is the visible verb ("Change"). `editOriginLabel` and `editDestinationLabel` name the buttons, by default the verb and the row's caption, "Change From" and "Change To". The new parameters follow the released seven, so positional calls still bind.
- **Client app banner (GAP-127):** `KozmosClientAppBanner(appName, actionLabel, onAction, modifier, promotionText, description, appIconUrl, onDismiss, dismissLabel)` draws Express's banner for the map shell's top bar, on its own solid surface. It shows the customer's promotion, the app's icon, name and description, and one action. Without an icon, or until it loads, the app's initial stands in for the 48dp icon. `onDismiss` adds a dismiss button, a 48dp target named by `dismissLabel`. TalkBack reads it as one group, the action and dismiss last, and it is not a pane. The banner never removes itself: the product does.
- **Theme fill (decision 59):** every prominent filled part draws the themed primary button's fill, theme 500 (`#135BEC`), with its foreground, white, on it in both themes: the primary Button and IconButton, FloatingActionButton, SplitButton, a checked Checkbox and its check, a checked Switch (a white thumb), Radio's dot, a selected Chip, the default Tag and Badge, the brand Counter, ToggleButton on, the filled primary LocationPin and its number, SaveLocationCard's saved disc, CategoryField's count pill, a filled MapControlButton, the Stepper's completed step, FloorSelector's selected level and result count, ManoeuvreCard's theme appearance and POIResultCard's selected number tab. Those already on theme 500 (Checkbox, Switch, Tag, FloatingActionButton, SplitButton, ToggleButton, FloorSelector's level, the pin) turn their dark-theme marks from black, which read 3.74:1, to white; those on the button token were `#0D44C2` in light and `#7EA2F6` under black in dark; the Stepper, FloorSelector's count, POIResultCard's tab and the theme ManoeuvreCard were theme 600 under black in dark. A count on the fill inverts, white with its number in the fill: FloorSelector's on its selected list level and the default Badge's counter. The themed button tokens change: the dark foreground from black to white, hover and focus to `#1051E8` and pressed to `#0D44C2` in both themes. Theme-coloured text, icons, edges, cursors and focus borders that drew theme 500 now draw theme 600, as React does (500 reads 3.13:1 on the dark sheet): Link, Icon Primary, the Toast action, Pagination, NavigationItem and its focus ring, Itinerary, DirectionStep, RouteSummary, RouteOptionCard, Timeline, POIResultCard's edge and dot, the AI search icon, the Listbox check, FileUpload, CategoryField's and CategoryTile's accent, the text fields' cursors and the Search, Textarea and OTPInput focus. Slider and RouteProgressRail move to 600 too. BottomNavigation takes Kozmos's colours, 600 when selected on background/100, in place of Material's purple (its indicator is still Material's pill behind the icon, where iOS and React fill the tile), and Radio draws its own mark: a 600 ring round the fill's dot. **Compose has no runtime colour override:** the fill is Pointr's `#135BEC` for every app; a client's Dashboard colour does not reach it yet (the web takes one override of theme 500).
- **States on the fill:** the filled `KozmosButton` (every emotion), `KozmosIconButton`'s filled variants, `KozmosFloatingActionButton`, both halves of `KozmosSplitButton` and `KozmosMapControlButton`'s filled state draw their pressed token while pressed (`#0D44C2` for the theme fill) and their focus or hover token while focused or hovered (`#1051E8`), in both themes, where Material's white ripple lightened the fill. A disabled or loading `KozmosButton` or `KozmosIconButton` (every variant), a disabled `KozmosCheckbox` and a disabled `KozmosSwitch` draw their own colours with the whole control at 50%, as React does, instead of Material's grey. `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton`, `KozmosFloatingActionButton` and `KozmosMapControlButton` take an optional `interactionSource: MutableInteractionSource?`, as Material's controls do: last, except on `KozmosButton`, where it comes before the trailing `content`.
- **Pin numbers read:** the accent `KozmosLocationPin`'s number is white on `#4134F1` in both themes (black read 2.99:1 in the dark), and a featured pin's number is the alert emotion's on-fill, black (white read 1.76:1 on the amber).
- **The filled controls draw themselves:** the filled `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton`'s filled variants, `KozmosFloatingActionButton`, both halves of `KozmosSplitButton` and a filled `KozmosMapControlButton` no longer use Material's `Button`, `FilledIconButton`, `FloatingActionButton` or `Surface`. Kozmos draws them on its own surface, a copy of Material 3's clickable surface with no indication, so no Material 3 version brings the ripple back over the pressed colour (1.3 and later ignore `LocalRippleTheme`). Glass, outline and ghost keep Material's ripple. `KozmosSplitButton` takes `mainInteractionSource` and `menuInteractionSource`.
- **One analytics local:** `com.kozmos.utils` no longer declares a second `LocalKozmosAnalytics` (a no-op), `KozmosAnalyticsEvent` and `KozmosAnalyticsTracker`, or `object KozmosAnalytics` with its `globalTracker` and `track(eventName, properties)`. Every component reads `com.kozmos.providers.LocalKozmosAnalytics`, and nothing read or called the `utils` copies, so a tracker provided through the `utils` local, or set as `KozmosAnalytics.globalTracker`, never received an event. `KozmosAnalyticsDeclarationsTest` fails if a second declaration of these names comes back.

### Migration notes

- **LocationPin's removed API (from 0.11.0):**
  - `KozmosLocationPinVariant` and `variant`, `KozmosLocationPinSize` and `size`, and `tint` are gone, with no replacement: the SDK's markers have a single look.
  - `offFloor` is gone (ruling 80). Don't give results on another level a marker: show the level switcher's counts (`KozmosFloorSelector`'s `showResultCounts`).
  - `label` no longer draws words beside the marker; it only names it. Pass `externalLabel` for visible words.
  - The number is no longer added to the marker's name: include it in `label`.
  - `markerContent` is now only the featured tile's logo; a Result and a place ignore it.
  - Positional calls past `modifier` must name their arguments, since the parameters moved.
  - **Positioning:** the composable's bounds now include its 48dp target, so the anchor is no longer at its bottom centre: a Result's tip sits 43 down its 48dp height, and a featured tile's or a place's dot centre 53 down its 56. Drop any bottom-centre offset you placed the old pin with, and place it with `Modifier.kozmosLocationPinAt { … }`, which puts the anchor on the point.
  - Review screenshot baselines with pins.
- A result that already passes `summary` now draws it: its card is taller. Review screenshot baselines and any layout that assumes a fixed row height, or leave `summary` unset to keep the old card.
- A product UI test that finds a result row with `onNodeWithContentDescription(...)` no longer matches: use `onNodeWithText(...)` with the same words, `substring = true` for one phrase. One that finds the row by a single drawn word in the merged tree, `onNodeWithText("Pharmacy")`, needs `substring = true`, or `useUnmergedTree = true` to find the drawn text itself.
- **Service chips are restyled.** `poi.services` draw as the details card's information chips: control-radius corners in place of pills, under a regular muted heading. A service with a known `iconName` now draws its icon, and TalkBack hears each chip as one stop in a list.
- **Strip actions honour `pressed` and `loading`.** A pressed action is filled and selected. A loading one cannot be pressed and says `loadingLabel` ("Loading" by default), so pass a translated one.
- **Icon names that drew the stand-in now draw their icon.** `KozmosIcon(name = "phone")` and the other 17 names above drew the Info glyph; a details chip with one of them drew nothing.
- **Summary colours and marks change.** Toned summary values are darker in light and lighter in dark, and the Material star, wheelchair, leaf and clock give way to Pointr's artwork. Review screenshot baselines for details cards and for screens that use these icon names.
- **RouteSummary's navigation layout.** Named calls and a trailing `progress` lambda compile and draw as before. `locationText` and `actions` sit before `progress`, so a fully positional call that passed `progress` eleventh must name it, `progress = …`. Leaving `onEndRoute` out now compiles and draws no End: pass it wherever End should show. In the estimate layout, a fully positional call that passed `transportModeIcon` eighth must name it too.
- **RouteSummary's estimate layout.** Pass translated `endRouteLabel` and `startNavigationLabel`: TalkBack said "End route" and Start read "Start Navigation" in every language. The active row's End now takes 48dp, so review screenshot baselines for it.
- **Map shell.** Where the credits had too little room, the shell now draws them whole without the Pointr logo, and it may hide the bottom corners on a phone with a tall top-bar card. Review screenshot baselines for those layouts. The extreme case, credits that do not fit even without the corners, still reaches the top bar's card: decision 58 rules out clipping them.
- **Progress colours change.** `KozmosProgress` draws theme 600 on background 200, so review screenshot baselines for anything that draws a progress bar, `KozmosEmptyState`'s included.
- Supply translated words for the new labels: `changeLabel` (and the endpoint captions it is joined with), `dismissLabel`, and the empty state's progress `label` and `valueText`.
- **Theme colours change (decision 59).** In light, the button-token parts move from `#0D44C2` to `#135BEC`; in dark, filled parts move from `#7EA2F6` or `#5887F3` with black on them to `#135BEC` with white. Pressed, focused, disabled and loading looks change too: a disabled outline or ghost button is its own colour at 50%, border included, not grey text, and a disabled `KozmosSwitch`'s label is its own colour at 50%. Review screenshot baselines. A part of your own that draws `primitivesColorsTheme500` as text or an edge reads 3.13:1 on the dark sheet: use `primitivesColorsTheme600`.
- **Focus rings sit outside the control.** A parent that clips its children clips the ring: leave 4dp around a focusable fill.
- **No hover lift.** A filled `KozmosButton` or `KozmosFloatingActionButton` no longer rises on a mouse hover (Material raised them 1dp and 8dp); its colour changes instead.
- **Material's colours change inside `KozmosThemeProvider`.** A part of your own that reads `MaterialTheme.colorScheme`, or a Material component left to its default colours, now draws Kozmos's: theme 600 where it drew purple, background/0 where Material tinted a lifted surface, foreground/100 words, and Kozmos's greys for outlines and dividers. Review screenshot baselines for those screens.
- **Analytics comes from `com.kozmos.providers` only.** An import of `com.kozmos.utils.LocalKozmosAnalytics`, `KozmosAnalyticsEvent`, `KozmosAnalyticsTracker` or `KozmosAnalytics` no longer compiles. Import the `com.kozmos.providers` names and provide your tracker with `CompositionLocalProvider(LocalKozmosAnalytics provides { event -> … })`; its event carries `component`, `eventName` and `properties` (`Map<String, String>?`). There is no global tracker: provide one where your composition starts.

## 0.6.0 — repository snapshot

Native source changes since the repository snapshot tagged `@kozmos-ds/react@0.5.0`. This entry does not announce a 0.6.0 tag or completed release validation. The web package has its own [changelog](../react/CHANGELOG.md).

### Added and changed

- **Map status pill:** `KozmosMapStatusPill` provides Neutral, Progress, Success, Danger and Warning tones, optional custom marks, and Off/Polite/Assertive live-region policies. Warning uses the named alert fill/on-fill pair. Words wrap instead of being cut; the default minimum height is 48dp. The product supplies the status text and chooses when it changes.
- **Travel-time bands:** `KozmosTravelTimeBand.forDuration(durationSeconds)` classifies a non-negative finite duration as Nearby, 1–2, 2–5, 5–10 or more than 10 minutes. Result cards use a band only when it is supplied on the travel estimate, with Nearby in the success colour. Without a band they retain the exact duration label; details continue to show the exact duration.
- **Result-card numbering:** `numbered` opts a card or list into displaying the supplied `resultIndex` in an inside-card, top-start tab. The tab follows layout direction and leaves font-scaled clearance above the title. Featured takes precedence over a number, and a number over a badge. Numbers are quiet at rest and filled when selected; Featured uses amber with dark words and a star; badges use a quiet grey treatment. An unselected Featured card has an amber edge; the selected card's theme-colour edge takes precedence.
- **Numbered map pins:** a non-Featured numbered pin on the current floor is outlined at rest and filled when selected. Off-floor pins retain a dashed outline, including when selected. Selected pins also grow; their selection semantics remain separate from whether the pin is enabled or actionable.
- **Floor switcher:** `KozmosFloorSelectorVariant.Collapsible` adds the SDK-style tile and expanding level column. `userFloor` and `userFloorLabel` support a visitor-level marker and product-supplied wording. Floor IDs remain selection keys; display labels do not replace them.
- **Location state:** `KozmosUserLocationState.HeadingPaused` represents heading remembered while the map is away from the visitor. `KozmosMapControlsGroup` renders its distinct paused-heading mark and description. Map-state transitions remain the product's responsibility.
- **Map controls and navigation:** map controls use a 48dp base surface, no border in either theme, and the map-control elevation role. Rail labels use `labelSmall` (11sp in the default type scale), 14sp line height and a two-line limit. Bottom-navigation labels also use `labelSmall`; custom product typography still applies.
- **Hosted panels and glass text:** browse and route-preview components reuse the map shell panel's surface instead of painting another fill. Their first rows account for the panel's existing top inset and grip clearance. The public `LocalKozmosSurfaceStyle` signal lets participating muted text use foreground ink on glass. Standalone browse surfaces retain their own fill.
- **POI detail header:** favourite and bookmark actions are controlled icon toggles beside close, using the supplied action labels and states. Other actions remain below the header in caller order. The header omits the logo box when there is no logo, and uses the name's initial while supplied artwork loads or fails. Top spacing aligns with side spacing where possible without colliding with the grip target.
- **Manoeuvre disclosure:** `KozmosManoeuvreCard` now displays the full instruction by default; `instructionLines` opts into truncation. Focus requests follow the disappearing part: to Hide when opening, and back to the instruction when closing. The 0.5.0 positional signature is retained through an overload, and the itinerary can remain a trailing lambda.
- **Icons:** `KozmosIcon(name = "bluetooth-off")` maps to Material's BluetoothDisabled. Status-pill examples use an explicit walking mark for walking guidance; progress still defaults to the spinner.

### Migration notes

- Add `KozmosUserLocationState.HeadingPaused` and `KozmosFloorSelectorVariant.Collapsible` to exhaustive `when` expressions over those enums. The paused-heading wire value is `"heading-paused"`. Handle the location button's existing callback in the product to resume heading; Kozmos does not perform that SDK transition itself.
- Pass floors in the desired display order. The selector **does not sort** them. Supply highest-to-lowest order if the up chevron should mean a higher physical level; `userFloor` marks a level without selecting it.
- Set `numbered = true` only when the list should match numbered map markers. Supply matching, one-based `resultIndex` values yourself; cards do not assign or renumber them. A custom `selectionLabel` replaces the generated accessible description, so include the number in that label if needed.
- Set `travelEstimate.band` explicitly to opt into banded result text. Supply translated `travelTimeBandLabels`; the helper classifies seconds, not already-rounded labels. Exactly 2, 5 and 10 minutes remain in the lower band; negative or non-finite durations produce `null`.
- Pass `instructionLines = 2` to retain the old manoeuvre instruction limit. Without it, allow the surrounding layout to grow. A limit under one also means no limit.
- Review screenshot baselines for tabs, pins, control surfaces, labels, hosted fills and detail headers. Native selected-card edges are not the web's separate selection ring.
- Rebuild consuming apps against the exact source revision and review positional calls to other composables. The compatibility overload above is specific to `KozmosManoeuvreCard`; it is **not** a guarantee that all 0.5.0 native call sites remain source-compatible. The broader compatibility follow-up is not included.

### Limits and validation scope

- This source review does not establish human TalkBack behaviour. Compose focus requests and semantics tests are not proof that TalkBack will follow the intended target; verify disclosure and focus recovery with the product's actual itinerary on a device.
- With empty text and no host size constraint, the status pill's remaining live-region box has no minimum size. The first empty-to-populated announcement remains a validation gap; do not rely on it as the sole way to communicate a critical status.
- Glass currently supplies tint and edge, not backdrop blur. Compose's elevation uses the platform shadow model, not the web/iOS three-layer shadow. These are intentional platform limitations, not exact visual parity claims.
- Dedicated Turn Back U-turn and Wayfinding Unavailable marks are not supplied here. Use an appropriate custom icon, or `icon = null`; do not treat the default warning triangle as those dedicated marks.
- Held follow-ups [#160](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/160), [#164](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/164), [#166](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/166) and [#168](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/168) are outside this candidate. Their proposed fixes are not claimed here.
