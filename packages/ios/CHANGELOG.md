# Changelog: Kozmos for iOS (SwiftUI)

Kozmos is consumed from this repository's `packages/ios` source as the `Kozmos` Swift package. It is not a published native registry release. Pin a reviewed repository commit when adopting these changes; the version headings below follow the web release plan, not a separate native package publication.

## Unreleased — repository source

Native source changes on `main` after the 0.6.0 snapshot below. Nothing here is tagged.

### Fixed

- **Twelve more parts take a tap across the whole of what they draw:** a plain button takes a tap only where its label draws something, and a frame or padding over a clear background adds nothing to its target, or to the frame VoiceOver outlines, without a content shape. Measured with XCUI, each took a tap only on its words or marks: `KozmosMultiSelect`'s clear and chevron (12.3pt square and 13.3 by 8pt, in 36pt frames), an inactive `KozmosPaginationLink` (its digit, 7 by 18pt, in a 36pt frame), an unselected `KozmosSegmentedControl` segment (its word, 38 by 17pt, of 117 by 44pt), an unselected `KozmosListbox` row (its words, 43.3 by 18pt of a 312 by 34pt row), an idle `KozmosNavigationItem` (its icon and label, 85.7 by 18pt of a 320 by 44pt side item), an unselected `KozmosBottomNavigation` tile (38.3 by 37.7pt of 64 by 64pt), `KozmosRadioGroupItem` and `KozmosCheckbox` (21pt tall with a label, of a 44pt row; without one a radio was its ring and a checkbox its box, 21pt square), `KozmosSwitch` (its track, 26pt tall with its edge, and its label's words; without a label its track, 46 by 26pt), `KozmosAccordionTrigger` (its title and chevron, 18pt tall, of a 50pt row), `KozmosRating`'s thumbs (their 40pt circles, in 44pt frames) and `KozmosColorPicker`'s format box (its word, 56 by 18pt, of 72 by 36pt). Each now takes a tap across its shape, the rounded rectangle, circle or row it draws, so the pointer's hover on an iPad keeps that shape too, and VoiceOver outlines all of it. Where the part is meant to be a 44pt target it is now one, the platform's minimum: the MultiSelect's clear and chevron are 44pt square, as `KozmosCombobox`'s and React's Combobox's are (React's MultiSelect draws 28px buttons, Compose's 36dp `IconButton`s); a page link is 44pt square and Previous and Next are 44pt tall, as React's touch links are (Compose's are 36dp); a list row is 44pt tall at least, as React's rows are since decision 115 (Compose's are 36dp); a radio or checkbox without a label is 44pt square, its mark at the leading edge (React's control is 20px plus its label; Compose's radio row is full width and 44dp tall, its checkbox row 44dp tall and 32dp wide without a label); a thumb is a 44pt circle, as React's 44px button is (Compose's is 40dp). The format box keeps React's 36px. `KozmosPagination` now wraps, as React's `flex-wrap` row does. With 44pt links, Code Connect's Ellipsis composition (Previous, 1, 2, 3, the ellipsis, 10, Next) is 385pt, where it was 353pt. That is wider than the 370pt column of a 402pt phone inside 16pt margins, and a row that could not wrap squeezed Previous from 88 to 70pt, breaking its word. Each part now keeps the size it asks for, a part that would pass the end of the row starts the next one, and each row is centred, as React's are; both of the layout's passes arrange the row for the width it was offered, so one rounded to the screen's pixels cannot narrow a part or push one onto the next row. A row that fits draws as before for Kozmos's own parts, but a `Spacer()` or a `.frame(maxWidth: .infinity)` a product puts inside `KozmosPagination` now keeps its own width, where the HStack stretched it. What changes on screen: the active page link's tint and edge grow from 36 to 44pt; the pagination row is 8pt taller and up to 8pt wider for each page link (the ellipsis, Previous and Next keep their widths), and a row wider than its space wraps, so in that 370pt column the Ellipsis composition puts Next on a second row; a list row with no description grows from 34 to 44pt, so a Combobox's or MultiSelect's list shows fewer such rows before it scrolls; the MultiSelect's chevron sits 4pt nearer the field's end and its clear 4pt further in, where `KozmosCombobox` puts its own, leaving 8pt less width for typing, and the field stays 48pt tall, where Figma and React draw 44pt, each target reaching 4pt above and below its row; a radio or checkbox without a label takes 24pt more width beside it. Colours, glyphs and every other size are unchanged. `InteractionTests` checks every target's size at the default text size. It also taps inside the part, clear of its words and marks, for the MultiSelect's clear and chevron, two page links, a segment, two list rows, the side and rail navigation items, a bottom navigation tile, a radio and a checkbox without a label, the accordion trigger and the format box. The rest are checked by size alone: Previous and Next, the top navigation item, the labelled radio, checkbox and switch, the switch without a label, and Rating's thumbs (a tap between a thumb's 40pt and 44pt circles is within SwiftUI's touch tolerance). The MultiSelect, page link, segment, list row, navigation item, bottom navigation, selection control and accordion tests run again at accessibility3 right to left, where grown words and marks can take the tap and the size check is the proof. A further test lays the Ellipsis composition in a 370pt column, at the default text size and at accessibility3 right to left, and checks that Previous and Next keep the size they have on their own, that every part stays inside the column, that Next is on a second row, that no two parts overlap, that the rows stay inside the size the row reports, and that Previous comes first in reading order. Each test failed on the code before its fix. `KozmosPOIDetailPanel`'s outlined actions are left as they are: a tap 7pt from an icon-only action's 1pt edge and its glyph, and one 7pt from a labelled action's edge and 6pt from its word, still landed on the unfixed code, inside SwiftUI's touch tolerance. The middle of a labelled action's 16pt side padding, about 8pt from both, was not tried, and an iPad pointer, which has no touch tolerance, can miss between the edge and the label.

- **The batching analytics modifier delivers events as React's `AnalyticsProvider` does:** `.kozmosAnalytics(batchDelayMs:onDispatch:)` never called `onDispatch`. It put a `KozmosAnalytics` engine in the environment as an `environmentObject`, which no part reads: every part sends to the `\.kozmosAnalytics` tracker. It now puts a tracker that feeds its engine in `\.kozmosAnalytics` for the view it modifies, so every part inside sends to it, and it keeps one engine for as long as that view is in the hierarchy (it made a new one, with an empty queue, each time the view above redrew).
  - **A batch is delivered `batchDelayMs` after its first event** (2000 by default), with every event sent meanwhile, in the order sent, as React's timer runs. The engine restarted its timer on every event, which made it a debounce: one event a second for five seconds was delivered once, at six. An event after a delivery opens the next batch.
  - **What is waiting when the view leaves the hierarchy is delivered then,** as React's provider delivers on unmount, and so is what a `KozmosAnalytics` of your own holds when it is released. Its `deinit` captured `[weak self]`, which is already nil there, so those events were dropped.
  - A nested modifier takes the events of the parts inside it, and the one above it gets none of them, as a nested React provider does. A tracker set inside it with `.environment(\.kozmosAnalytics, …)` takes its own subtree's events in the same way. Like React, it does not deliver early when the app moves to the background.
  - `onDispatch` is called on the main thread. `trackEvent` and `flushQueue()` act at once on the main thread, and from another thread go through the main queue; `flushQueue()` delivered later on the main queue even when called there. With no `onDispatch`, a `KozmosAnalytics` prints each event of a batch as the default tracker prints it, as React's provider logs a batch it has nowhere to send; it dropped them. The engine no longer publishes a change for each event, so a view that observes it no longer redraws for each one.
  - The signatures are unchanged. - **Pressed and focused fills (decision 59):** a filled part draws its own emotion's Primary Buttons pressed and focus tokens, with that emotion's ink, as React and Compose do: `KozmosButton` `.default` (themed: `#0D44C2` pressed, `#1051E8` focused), `.destructive` (danger) and `.secondary` (neutral; its foreground/100 words are unchanged), and any of them with an `emotion`; `KozmosIconButton` `.default`, `.destructive` and `.secondary`; `KozmosFloatingActionButton`; `KozmosSplitButton`'s action half; and a `.filled` `KozmosMapControlButton` while it is on. They drew SwiftUI's highlight over the idle fill. Layout, hit area and traits are unchanged, and the parts keep the pointer hover effect. A `.filled` map control that is off dims to 75% under a press, as `.plain` does. SplitButton's menu half keeps SwiftUI's press: a `Menu` does not run a custom button style's pressed state.
- **Loading:** a loading Button, IconButton or MapControlButton is drawn at 50%, as a disabled one is; it was drawn at full strength.
- **Badge:** the counter on a default Badge inverts, white with its number in the theme fill, in both appearances; it was a black pill in the dark. A destructive Badge is unchanged.

### Migration notes

- **Analytics:** `.kozmosAnalytics(batchDelayMs:onDispatch:)` now calls `onDispatch`, with every Kozmos part's events inside the modified view, which it never did. Check what your `onDispatch` sends before taking this commit. A view inside it that sets `.environment(\.kozmosAnalytics, …)` keeps its own tracker for its parts. Code that called `flushQueue()` and read the result on a later turn of the main queue now gets it before `flushQueue()` returns.
- **LocationPin (decisions 79 and 80):** the initializer changes in a way that breaks source (a 0.x minor).
  - `variant:` with `KozmosLocationPinVariant`, `size:` with `KozmosLocationPinSize`, and `tint:` are removed, with no replacement: the SDK's markers have one look. Delete the arguments.
  - `offFloor:` is removed, with no replacement. Don't mark results on other levels: draw markers for the level in view only, and let the level switcher count the rest (`KozmosFloorSelector`'s `showResultCounts`). VoiceOver no longer hears "On another floor".
  - The frame is centred on the anchor. Place a marker with `.kozmosLocationPinAt(point)` and drop any offset that put a circle's centre or bottom on the point.
  - A featured marker draws no accent disc and no star: pass the place's logo in `markerContent`. `markerContent` on a result or a place is ignored.
  - The number is no longer added to the marker's name: include it in `label`.
  - `label` is now the spoken name only; pass `externalLabel` for the words drawn beside the marker.
  - Review screenshot baselines with pins.
- **ManoeuvreCard (decision 74):** the closed card is 39pt shorter and the open card 16pt shorter. Review screenshot baselines, and anything placed under the card by its height.
- **Map shell (GAP-085):** panel content you host ends 16pt above the panel's bottom edge, or above the sheet's bottom safe area, and a fitted panel is 16pt taller. Remove a bottom padding of your own that stood in for it, and review screenshot baselines for panels and sheets. Content that drew a fill to the panel's edge now stops 16 short of it: let the panel's surface (`panelSurface`) draw it.
- Toned summary values are darker in light and lighter in dark. Review screenshot baselines for details cards that set `tone`.
- A result that already passes `summary` now draws it: its card is taller. Review screenshot baselines and any layout that assumes a fixed row height, or leave `summary` unset to keep the old card.
- **RouteSummary:** existing navigation calls compile and draw as before. `onEndRoute` keeps its place and `progress` stays required, so a trailing closure still fills `progress`. Leaving `onEndRoute` out now compiles and draws no End: pass it wherever End should show. Pass translated `endRouteLabel` and `startNavigationLabel` to the estimate layout: VoiceOver said "End route" and Start read "Start Navigation" in every language.
- **Map shell:** where the credits had too little room, the shell now draws them whole without the Pointr logo, and it may hide the bottom corners on a phone with a tall top-bar card. Review screenshot baselines for those layouts. The extreme case, credits that do not fit even without the corners, still reaches the top bar's card: decision 58 rules out clipping them.
- **Arrows and Progress:** Pagination, Breadcrumb and RoutePreviewPanel arrows now point the other way right to left, and `KozmosProgress` draws the theme's colour, not the system blue. Review screenshot baselines for right-to-left screens and for anything that draws a progress bar, `KozmosEmptyState`'s included.

## 0.6.0 — repository snapshot

Native source changes since the repository snapshot tagged `@kozmos-ds/react@0.5.0`. This entry does not announce a 0.6.0 tag or completed release validation. The web package has its own [changelog](../react/CHANGELOG.md).

### Added and changed

- **Map status pill:** `KozmosMapStatusPill` provides neutral, progress, success, danger and warning tones, optional custom marks, and off/polite/assertive announcement policies. Warning uses the named alert fill/on-fill pair. Words wrap instead of being cut; the default minimum height is 48pt. The product supplies the status text and chooses when it changes.
- **Travel-time bands:** `KozmosTravelTimeBand(durationSeconds:)` classifies a non-negative finite duration as Nearby, 1–2, 2–5, 5–10 or more than 10 minutes. Result cards use a band only when it is supplied on the travel estimate, with Nearby in the success colour. Without a band they retain the exact duration label; details continue to show the exact duration.
- **Result-card numbering:** `numbered` opts a card or list into displaying `resultIndex` in an inside-card, top-leading tab. The tab follows layout direction and leaves clearance above the title. Featured takes precedence over a number, and a number over a badge. Numbers are quiet at rest and filled when selected; Featured uses amber with dark words and a star; badges use a quiet grey treatment. An unselected Featured card has an amber edge; the selected card's theme-colour edge takes precedence.
- **Numbered map pins:** a non-Featured numbered pin on the current floor is outlined at rest and filled when selected. Off-floor pins retain a dashed outline, including when selected. Selected pins also grow. Selection is exposed in accessibility traits even when the pin is disabled or has no selection callback; this does not make it actionable.
- **Floor switcher:** the existing `.collapsible` variant now uses the SDK-style tile and expanding level column. `userFloor`, `userFloorLabel` and `expandHint` support a visitor-level marker and product-supplied wording. Floor IDs remain selection keys; display labels do not replace them.
- **Location state:** `KozmosUserLocationState.headingPaused` represents heading remembered while the map is away from the visitor. `KozmosMapControlsGroup` renders its distinct paused-heading mark and description. Map-state transitions remain the product's responsibility.
- **Map controls and navigation:** map controls use a 48pt base surface, no border in either theme, and the map-control elevation role. Rail labels use scalable `caption2` text on nominal 14pt lines, with a two-line limit, and the rail adopts the side-menu treatment.
- **Hosted panels:** browse and route-preview components reuse the map shell panel's surface instead of painting another fill. Their first rows account for the panel's existing top inset and grip clearance. Detail-header buttons align to the side inset where space permits, retaining extra clearance when they would collide with the grip target.
- **Glass text:** the public `EnvironmentValues.kozmosSurfaceStyle` signal describes the surrounding Kozmos surface. Participating muted text switches to foreground ink on glass. Standalone browse surfaces retain their own fill.
- **Manoeuvre disclosure:** `KozmosManoeuvreCard` now displays the full instruction by default; `instructionLines` opts into truncation. Disclosure changes request accessibility focus on the replacement part only when focus belonged to the part being removed.
- **Icons:** `KozmosIcon("bluetooth-off")` uses the Pointr outline. Status-pill examples use an explicit walking mark for walking guidance; progress still defaults to the spinner.

### Migration notes

- Add `.headingPaused` to exhaustive switches over `KozmosUserLocationState`; its raw value is `"heading-paused"`. Handle the location button's existing callback in the product to resume heading. Kozmos does not perform that SDK transition itself.
- Pass floors in the desired display order. The selector **does not sort** them. Supply highest-to-lowest order if the up chevron should mean a higher physical level; `userFloor` marks a level without selecting it.
- Set `numbered: true` only when the list should match numbered map markers. Supply matching, one-based `resultIndex` values yourself; cards do not assign or renumber them. A custom `selectionLabel` replaces the generated accessible description, so include the number in that label if needed.
- Set `travelEstimate.band` explicitly to opt into banded result text. Supply translated `travelTimeBandLabels`; the helper classifies seconds, not already-rounded labels. Exactly 2, 5 and 10 minutes remain in the lower band; negative or non-finite durations produce `nil`.
- Pass `instructionLines: 2` to retain the old manoeuvre instruction limit. Without it, allow the surrounding layout to grow. A limit under one also means no limit.
- Review screenshot baselines for tabs, pins, control surfaces, rail labels, hosted fills and header spacing. Native selected-card edges are not the web's separate selection ring.
- Rebuild consuming apps against the exact source revision and review their initializer calls. This candidate does **not** guarantee all 0.5.0 native call sites remain source-compatible; the broader native compatibility follow-up is not included.

### Limits and validation scope

- This source review does not establish human VoiceOver behaviour. The manoeuvre disclosure requests focus on the itinerary container when opening and the instruction when closing; verify the resulting experience with the product's actual itinerary and VoiceOver on a device.
- Glass uses the system material plus Kozmos tint and edge. Its blur and saturation are system-defined, not pixel-identical to the web. Reduce Transparency uses a solid surface; the stronger glass-text treatment remains.
- Dedicated Turn Back U-turn and Wayfinding Unavailable marks are not supplied here. Use an appropriate custom icon, or `showsIcon: false`; do not treat the default warning triangle as those dedicated marks.
- The iOS harness is configured to run the complete test target on a pinned simulator. A macOS `swift test` result alone does not validate iOS-only tests, and this changelog is not proof that the final candidate passed that simulator run.
- Held follow-ups [#160](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/160), [#164](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/164), [#166](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/166) and [#168](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/168) are outside this candidate. Their proposed fixes are not claimed here.
