# Changelog: Kozmos for iOS (SwiftUI)

Kozmos is consumed from this repository's `packages/ios` source as the `Kozmos` Swift package. It is not a published native registry release. Pin a reviewed repository commit when adopting these changes; the version headings below follow the web release plan, not a separate native package publication.

## Unreleased — repository source

Native source changes on `main` after the 0.6.0 snapshot below. Nothing here is tagged.

### Fixed

- **POI details summary tones:** a fact's value and icon use the emotion's text role for success, warning and danger (`semanticsEmotionSuccessText`, `semanticsEmotionAlertText`, `semanticsEmotionDangerText`) and `primitivesColorsTheme600` for brand, as the web's summary names them. Each now reads at 4.5:1 or more on the card's white and the sheet's grey in both appearances. The fill colours they used read as low as 1.57:1 (warning on the sheet), and brand read at 3.74:1 on black.
- **Map credits are never clipped (GAP-135, decision 58):** `KozmosAdaptiveMapShell` no longer shrinks the attribution into a scroll region when an opened top-bar card and the sheet leave it too little room. The Pointr logo gives way first: `KozmosMapAttribution` leaves out its brand and the credits keep their full height. The panel's sizing still reserves the whole attribution (at most half the band below the top bar), so the logo giving way never moves the sheet. The room counts the lift above the bottom corners when they leave no 128pt middle slot. Credits still too tall to sit above the corners send the corners away (hidden, not hit-testable and hidden from VoiceOver, as when they do not fit), and the credits return to the bottom row. The logo comes back once it fits again: while it has given way, the shell measures an unseen, unreachable copy of the whole attribution.
- **Arrows follow the reading direction:** `KozmosPaginationPrevious` and `KozmosPaginationNext`, `KozmosBreadcrumb`'s separator and `KozmosRoutePreviewPanel`'s Back draw `chevron.backward`, `chevron.forward` and `arrow.backward`, which SwiftUI mirrors right to left. They pointed left or right in either direction.
- **Progress takes the theme:** `KozmosProgress` tints its bar with `primitivesColorsTheme600` through `.tint`, as React's bar is drawn. It drew the system blue, whatever the theme, through the deprecated `LinearProgressViewStyle(tint:)`.

### Added and changed

- **SplitButton's menu half keeps the system press (decision 64):** a deliberate platform difference, not a gap. `KozmosSplitButton`'s menu half is a `Menu`, which does not run a custom button style's pressed state, so it draws SwiftUI's own press. The action half draws the themed pressed token, `#0D44C2`, as React and Compose draw both halves.
- **Emotion states step away from the page (decision 60):** a filled emotion part's hover and focus are one step, and its pressed two steps, further along the emotion's own ramp: darker in light, lighter in dark. This covers `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton` and every part drawn through `KozmosFillButtonStyle`.
  - Danger in light is `#B01736`, then `#8C132B`, then `#670E20` (the hover was `#D41C42`, lighter than the fill).
  - In dark, danger, success, alert and informative step lighter, 700 → 800 → 900. Light neutral presses to `#9095A2`, where it went lighter (`#E3E4E8`).
  - Dark neutral keeps darkening, `#464A53` → `#2E3138` → `#17191C`, because white reads 3.72:1 on its lighter step.
  - The colours come from `KozmosColors`, which `pnpm tokens:native:copy` regenerated.
- **Brand variant 1's 500 is `#4135F1` (decision 63):** `primitivesColorsThemeVariant1500`, the accent `KozmosLocationPin`'s fill, is lightened from `#4134F1` just enough to read 3:1 on the dark page (3.00:1). White on it reads 6.99:1.
- **Tabs draw React's raised segment (decision 65):**
  - `KozmosTabsList` is a background/100 track with the control radius.
  - The selected `KozmosTabsTrigger` is a background/0 segment 4pt inside it, its corner concentric (12pt in the 16pt track), raised by `semanticsElevationRaised`, with foreground/0 words.
  - The other tabs' words are foreground/400. All tabs use subheadline medium on one line.
  - Before, the list was background/0 with a 2pt theme-500 underline under semibold foreground/100 words; the other tabs were regular foreground/500. Nothing on tabs is the theme's colour now.
  - The track is 44pt tall, and all of it is each tab's target. A disabled tab is drawn at half, as React draws it. The initializers are unchanged.
  - Migration: review screenshot baselines for screens with tabs. A long label now truncates on one line instead of wrapping.
- **Result summaries and their language:** `KozmosPOIResultPresentation.summaryLanguage` names the language of `summary` when it differs from the interface's (GAP-125). `KozmosPOIResultCard` now draws `summary`, muted and two lines at most, after the location, as the web card does, and VoiceOver hears it after the location. When `nameLanguage` or `summaryLanguage` is set, the select row is spoken through a UIKit element whose label carries each tagged phrase's speech language, with the same words, traits, action and `kozmosPOIResultIdentifier` as the untagged row.
- **Container inset (GAP-134):** `KozmosContainer(inset:content:)` takes `KozmosContainerInset`, as React's `inset` does. `.window` pads the sides 16, 24 or 32pt with the width it is given (from 640 and from 1024pt), for a page; `.panel` keeps 16 at any width, for a sheet or side panel. It fills the width and pads the sides only, and several views stack top to bottom with no spacing, as a React Container's children do. `KozmosContainer(content:)` is unchanged: it still pads all four sides by the platform default.
- **Empty state progress (GAP-115):** `KozmosEmptyState` takes `progress: KozmosEmptyStateProgress(value:label:valueText:)`, a long wait it explains, such as a download. It is drawn under the description and above the action, at most 280pt wide: `label` above the bar, and `valueText` ("12 of 30 MB") beside it when given. `value` runs from 0 to 1; outside that it is clamped, and NaN is 0. VoiceOver hears one element that updates frequently: the label, then `valueText` or the rounded percentage. Both initializers take it, before the view builders.
- **RouteSummary actions and route preview (GAP-110, GAP-111, decision 57):** the navigation initializer is now `init<Actions: View>(destination:locationText:durationText:distanceText:arrivalText:endLabel:surface:presentation:destinationImage:onEndRoute:progress:actions:)`.
  - `actions` comes after `progress`: the journey's actions, Previous and Next in static wayfinding, Go and Details in the route preview. They are laid out in equal columns in reading order, as tall as the tallest, and a `KozmosButton` there fills its column, its label wrapping. The host owns what they do, when they are disabled, and announcing the new step.
  - `onEndRoute` is optional, `(() -> Void)? = nil`. Without it there is no End: the route preview, where `locationText` is the place's muted line under the destination. The preview passes `progress: { EmptyView() }`.
  - The estimate layout's initializers take `endRouteLabel` (End's accessible name) and `startNavigationLabel` (Start's label), as React's props of the same names, before `transportModeIcon`. They default to the words drawn before, "End route" and "Start Navigation".
- **Itinerary Change actions (GAP-104):** `KozmosItinerary` takes `onEditOrigin` and `onEditDestination`. Each ends its endpoint's row in a ghost Change button, drawn only when passed, at the row's trailing end on the name's first baseline; the host opens the endpoint for editing and moves focus there. `changeLabel` is the visible verb ("Change"). `editOriginLabel` and `editDestinationLabel` name the buttons, by default the verb and the row's caption, "Change From" and "Change To". The new parameters follow the released ones.
- **Client app banner (GAP-127):** `KozmosClientAppBanner(appName:actionLabel:promotionText:description:appIconURL:dismissLabel:onAction:onDismiss:)` draws Express's banner for the map shell's top bar, on its own solid surface. It shows the customer's promotion, the app's icon, name and description, and one action. Without an icon, or until it loads, the app's initial stands in for the 48pt icon. `onDismiss` adds a 44pt dismiss button named by `dismissLabel`. The banner never removes itself: the product does.
- **Theme fill (decision 59):** every prominent filled part draws the themed primary button's fill, theme 500 (`#135BEC`), with its foreground, white, on it in both appearances: the primary Button (the default variant, or a filled variant with `emotion: .themed`) and IconButton, FloatingActionButton, SplitButton, a checked Checkbox and its check, a checked Switch (a white thumb), Radio's dot (its ring is 600), a selected Chip, the default Tag and Badge, the brand Counter, FloorSelector's selected tile and result count, the filled primary LocationPin and its number, SaveLocationCard's saved disc, CategoryField's count pill, POIDetailPanel's primary and pressed actions, a filled MapControlButton, ToggleButton on, POIResultCard's selected number tab, the Stepper's completed step and the guidance theme appearance (the ManoeuvreCard). What changes depends on where a part was: those already on theme 500 (Checkbox, Switch, Tag, FloatingActionButton, SplitButton, ToggleButton, FloorSelector's tile, the pin) keep their colour and turn their dark-appearance marks from black, which read 3.74:1, to white; those on the button token (Button, IconButton, Chip, Badge, Counter, the saved disc, the count pill, POIDetailPanel's actions, MapControlButton) were `#0D44C2` in light and `#7EA2F6` under black in dark; FloorSelector's count, POIResultCard's tab, the Stepper and the theme ManoeuvreCard were theme 600 under black in dark. A count on the fill inverts, white with its number in the fill: FloorSelector's on its selected list tile and the default Badge's counter (it was a black pill in dark). The themed button tokens themselves change: the dark foreground from black to white, hover and focus to `#1051E8` and pressed to `#0D44C2` in both appearances (dark hover was `#5887F3`, dark pressed `#A4BEF9`, light pressed `#0B369C`). Theme-coloured text, icons, edges and rings that drew theme 500 now draw theme 600, as React does (500 reads 3.13:1 on the dark sheet and 3.74:1 on the black page): Link, brand Text, Icon primary, the Toast action, BottomNavigation, Pagination, NavigationItem and its focus ring, Itinerary, DirectionStep, RouteSummary, RouteOptionCard, Timeline, POIResultCard's edge and dot, the AI search icon, the Listbox check, Tree, FileUpload, the OTPInput focus, CategoryField's and CategoryTile's accent, Radio's ring and the `.background` ManoeuvreCard's glyph. Slider and RouteProgressRail move to 600 too. Outline, ghost and link Button and IconButton text take the Secondary Buttons token in place of theme 500, as React and Compose draw it. **SwiftUI has no runtime colour override:** the fill is Pointr's `#135BEC` for every app; a client's Dashboard colour does not reach it yet (the web takes one override of theme 500).
- **Text on a fill:** `KozmosTextTone.onEmphasis` is now the theme foreground, white in both appearances, for text on the theme fill. It was background/0, black in the dark, which reads 3.74:1 on the fill. A new `KozmosTextTone.onDanger` keeps background/0 for text on a danger fill (the Primary Buttons danger fill and emotional danger 600), where the dark appearance's light reds take black and not white.
- **Pressed and focused fills (decision 59):** a filled part draws its own emotion's Primary Buttons pressed and focus tokens, with that emotion's ink, as React and Compose do: `KozmosButton` `.default` (themed: `#0D44C2` pressed, `#1051E8` focused), `.destructive` (danger) and `.secondary` (neutral; its foreground/100 words are unchanged), and any of them with an `emotion`; `KozmosIconButton` `.default`, `.destructive` and `.secondary`; `KozmosFloatingActionButton`; `KozmosSplitButton`'s action half; and a `.filled` `KozmosMapControlButton` while it is on. They drew SwiftUI's highlight over the idle fill. Layout, hit area and traits are unchanged, and the parts keep the pointer hover effect. A `.filled` map control that is off dims to 75% under a press, as `.plain` does. SplitButton's menu half keeps SwiftUI's press: a `Menu` does not run a custom button style's pressed state.
- **Loading:** a loading Button, IconButton or MapControlButton is drawn at 50%, as a disabled one is; it was drawn at full strength.
- **Badge:** the counter on a default Badge inverts, white with its number in the theme fill, in both appearances; it was a black pill in the dark. A destructive Badge is unchanged.
- **LocationPin:** a filled accent pin's number is white in both appearances (it read 2.99:1 in the dark on `#4134F1`), and a featured pin's number is the alert's on-fill colour, black, whatever its tint (white read 1.77:1 on `#FAB735`).

### Migration notes

- Toned summary values are darker in light and lighter in dark. Review screenshot baselines for details cards that set `tone`.
- A result that already passes `summary` now draws it: its card is taller. Review screenshot baselines and any layout that assumes a fixed row height, or leave `summary` unset to keep the old card.
- **RouteSummary:** existing navigation calls compile and draw as before. `onEndRoute` keeps its place and `progress` stays required, so a trailing closure still fills `progress`. Leaving `onEndRoute` out now compiles and draws no End: pass it wherever End should show. Pass translated `endRouteLabel` and `startNavigationLabel` to the estimate layout: VoiceOver said "End route" and Start read "Start Navigation" in every language.
- **Map shell:** where the credits had too little room, the shell now draws them whole without the Pointr logo, and it may hide the bottom corners on a phone with a tall top-bar card. Review screenshot baselines for those layouts. The extreme case, credits that do not fit even without the corners, still reaches the top bar's card: decision 58 rules out clipping them.
- **Arrows and Progress:** Pagination, Breadcrumb and RoutePreviewPanel arrows now point the other way right to left, and `KozmosProgress` draws the theme's colour, not the system blue. Review screenshot baselines for right-to-left screens and for anything that draws a progress bar, `KozmosEmptyState`'s included.
- Supply translated words for the new labels: `changeLabel` (and the endpoint captions it is joined with), `dismissLabel`, and the empty state's progress `label` and `valueText`.
- **Theme colours change (decision 59).** In light, the button-token parts move from `#0D44C2` to `#135BEC` and the 600 parts from `#1051E8`; in dark, filled parts move from `#7EA2F6` or `#5887F3` with black on them to `#135BEC` with white. Review screenshot baselines. A view of your own that draws `primitivesColorsTheme500` as text or an edge reads 3.13:1 on the dark sheet: use `primitivesColorsTheme600`. A view that reads the themed button tokens directly gets white ink in dark and the new hover and pressed values.
- **`onEmphasis` on a danger fill:** text with `tone: .onEmphasis` on a danger fill is now white in the dark, which reads 3.39:1 there. Use `tone: .onDanger`. A `switch` over every `KozmosTextTone` needs a case for `.onDanger`.
- **Your `.buttonStyle(_:)` no longer reaches the filled parts,** which now set their own: `KozmosButton` with `variant: .default`, `.destructive` or `.secondary` (with or without an `emotion`), `KozmosIconButton` with `.default`, `.destructive` or `.secondary`, `KozmosFloatingActionButton`, `KozmosSplitButton`'s action half, and `KozmosMapControlButton` with `emphasis: .filled`, on or off. Outline, ghost, link and glass variants and SplitButton's menu still take it; a tinted MapControlButton keeps `.plain`.
- **Review image baselines:** loading parts are at half, pressed parts draw their pressed token, and a filled Button's label can rasterise a fraction of a pixel differently. A featured pin's number is now dark and an accent pin's white in the dark.

## 0.6.0 — repository snapshot

Native source changes since the repository snapshot tagged `@kozmos-ds/react@0.5.0`. This entry does not announce a 0.6.0 tag or completed release validation. The web package has its own [changelog](../react/CHANGELOG.md).

### Added and changed

- **Map status pill:** `KozmosMapStatusPill` provides neutral, progress, success, danger and warning tones, optional custom marks, and off/polite/assertive announcement policies. Warning uses the named alert fill/on-fill pair. Words wrap instead of being cut; the default minimum height is 48pt. The product supplies the status text and chooses when it changes.
- **Travel-time bands:** `KozmosTravelTimeBand(durationSeconds:)` classifies a non-negative finite duration as Nearby, 1–2, 2–5, 5–10 or more than 10 minutes. Result cards use a band only when it is supplied on the travel estimate, with Nearby in the success colour. Without a band they retain the exact duration label; details continue to show the exact duration.
- **Result-card numbering:** `numbered` opts a card or list into displaying `resultIndex` in an inside-card, top-leading tab. The tab follows layout direction and leaves clearance above the title. Featured takes precedence over a number, and a number over a badge. Numbers are quiet at rest and filled when selected; Featured uses amber with dark words and a star; badges use a quiet grey treatment. An unselected Featured card has an amber edge; the selected card's theme-colour edge takes precedence.
- **Numbered map pins:** a non-Featured numbered pin on the current floor is outlined at rest and filled when selected. Off-floor pins retain a dashed outline, including when selected. Selected pins also grow. Selection is exposed in accessibility traits even when the pin is disabled or has no selection callback; this does not make it actionable.
- **Floor switcher:** the existing `.collapsible` variant now uses the SDK-style tile and expanding level column. `userFloor`, `userFloorLabel` and `expandHint` support a visitor-level marker and product-supplied wording. Floor IDs remain selection keys; display labels do not replace them.
- **Location state:** `KozmosUserLocationState.headingPaused` represents heading remembered while the map is away from the visitor. `KozmosMapControlsGroup` renders its distinct paused-heading mark and description. Map-state transitions remain the product's responsibility.
- **Map controls and navigation:** map controls use a 48pt base surface, no border in either theme, and the map-control elevation role. Rail labels use scalable `caption2` text on nominal 14pt lines, with a two-line limit, and the rail adopts the side-menu treatment.
- **Hosted panels:** browse and route-preview components reuse the map shell panel's surface instead of painting another fill. Their first rows account for the panel's existing top inset and grip clearance. Detail-header buttons align to the side inset where space permits, retaining extra clearance when they would collide with the grip target.
- **Glass text:** the public `EnvironmentValues.kozmosSurfaceStyle` signal describes the surrounding Kozmos surface. Participating muted text switches to foreground ink on glass. Standalone browse surfaces retain their own fill.
- **Manoeuvre disclosure:** `KozmosManoeuvreCard` now displays the full instruction by default; `instructionLines` opts into truncation. Disclosure changes request accessibility focus on the replacement part only when focus belonged to the part being removed.
- **Icons:** `KozmosIcon("bluetooth-off")` uses the Pointr outline. Status-pill examples use an explicit walking mark for walking guidance; progress still defaults to the spinner.

### Migration notes

- Add `.headingPaused` to exhaustive switches over `KozmosUserLocationState`; its raw value is `"heading-paused"`. Handle the location button's existing callback in the product to resume heading. Kozmos does not perform that SDK transition itself.
- Pass floors in the desired display order. The selector **does not sort** them. Supply highest-to-lowest order if the up chevron should mean a higher physical level; `userFloor` marks a level without selecting it.
- Set `numbered: true` only when the list should match numbered map markers. Supply matching, one-based `resultIndex` values yourself; cards do not assign or renumber them. A custom `selectionLabel` replaces the generated accessible description, so include the number in that label if needed.
- Set `travelEstimate.band` explicitly to opt into banded result text. Supply translated `travelTimeBandLabels`; the helper classifies seconds, not already-rounded labels. Exactly 2, 5 and 10 minutes remain in the lower band; negative or non-finite durations produce `nil`.
- Pass `instructionLines: 2` to retain the old manoeuvre instruction limit. Without it, allow the surrounding layout to grow. A limit under one also means no limit.
- Review screenshot baselines for tabs, pins, control surfaces, rail labels, hosted fills and header spacing. Native selected-card edges are not the web's separate selection ring.
- Rebuild consuming apps against the exact source revision and review their initializer calls. This candidate does **not** guarantee all 0.5.0 native call sites remain source-compatible; the broader native compatibility follow-up is not included.

### Limits and validation scope

- This source review does not establish human VoiceOver behaviour. The manoeuvre disclosure requests focus on the itinerary container when opening and the instruction when closing; verify the resulting experience with the product's actual itinerary and VoiceOver on a device.
- Glass uses the system material plus Kozmos tint and edge. Its blur and saturation are system-defined, not pixel-identical to the web. Reduce Transparency uses a solid surface; the stronger glass-text treatment remains.
- Dedicated Turn Back U-turn and Wayfinding Unavailable marks are not supplied here. Use an appropriate custom icon, or `showsIcon: false`; do not treat the default warning triangle as those dedicated marks.
- The iOS harness is configured to run the complete test target on a pinned simulator. A macOS `swift test` result alone does not validate iOS-only tests, and this changelog is not proof that the final candidate passed that simulator run.
- Held follow-ups [#160](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/160), [#164](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/164), [#166](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/166) and [#168](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/168) are outside this candidate. Their proposed fixes are not claimed here.
