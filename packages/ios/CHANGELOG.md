# Changelog: Kozmos for iOS (SwiftUI)

Kozmos is consumed from this repository's `packages/ios` source as the `Kozmos` Swift package. It is not a published native registry release. Pin a reviewed repository commit when adopting these changes; the version headings below follow the web release plan, not a separate native package publication.

## Unreleased — repository source

Native source changes on `main` after the 0.6.0 snapshot below. Nothing here is tagged.

### Fixed

- **The breadcrumb says which crumb is the current page, and reads no separators:** VoiceOver heard `KozmosBreadcrumb`'s last crumb only as a dimmed button, and read each separator's symbol as "Forward" between the crumbs. React's breadcrumb marks its page `aria-current="page"` and hides its separators. The current crumb is now selected, the platform's word for the current item of a set, as `KozmosBottomNavigation`'s current tab is, and the separators are decoration. VoiceOver walks "Home", "Terminal 2" and "Gate 14, selected, dimmed"; a double tap on an earlier crumb still calls `onSelect`. The look and the initializer are unchanged. `KozmosBreadcrumbAccessibilityTests` reads the trail with accessibility automation on.
- **Headings grow with the reader's text size:** `KozmosHeading` drew each level at a fixed `.system(size:)`, 36pt for h1 down to 16pt for h6, which Dynamic Type never touches. At the largest text sizes the body grew around headings that stayed as they were, where React's heading is sized in rem and Compose's in sp. Each level now grows with Dynamic Type along the curve of the text style nearest its size: h1 as the large title, h2 the title, h3 title 2, h4 title 3, h5 the headline and h6 the callout. At the default text size every level is exactly the size it was, and the levels keep their order at every size. The initializer, weight, colour and heading level are unchanged. `KozmosHeadingDynamicTypeTests` measures every level at the default size and at accessibility 3.
- **Radios and toggle buttons say their state:** VoiceOver heard `KozmosRadioGroupItem` and `KozmosToggleButton` as plain buttons, their names and nothing more; which radio was chosen and whether a toggle was on showed only in the dot and the fill. React's are Radix's radio with `aria-checked` and toggle with `aria-pressed`. The chosen radio and a toggle that is on are now selected, the trait VoiceOver says as "selected" in the visitor's own language, as `KozmosChip`'s `aria-pressed` and the tabs' `aria-selected` already are. A double tap still chooses the radio and turns the toggle on and off, and the trait follows. The look and the initializers are unchanged. `KozmosRadioToggleAccessibilityTests` reads the tree with accessibility automation on.
- **A navigation item's icon is the item's colour:** a `KozmosIcon` in a `KozmosNavigationItem` kept its own foreground/100, because the item did not set `kozmosIconHostInk`. So a selected rail item drew a theme/600 label under a near-black icon, and an item at rest a foreground/400 label under it. React's icon is `currentColor` inside the item, and Compose's `KozmosIcon` follows `LocalContentColor`. The item now gives its icon its own colour, as `KozmosButton` gives its icon its ink: in a rail, foreground/400 at rest and theme/600 selected; in a row, foreground/100 and theme/600; foreground/500 disabled. It gives its trailing content foreground/500, the colour it already drew it in. Icons that are not `KozmosIcon`, or that set a colour of their own, draw as before. The NavigationItem page's SwiftUI rail example draws `KozmosIcon` ("home-line", "navigation-pointer-01") now that it takes the item's colour. `KozmosNavigationItemIconInkTests` reads the icons' ink.
- **A toast tells VoiceOver it has appeared:** `KozmosToast` posted nothing, so VoiceOver said nothing when one appeared, and by default it was gone again in three seconds. React's toast is Radix's, which announces the toast's words through an assertive live region, `aria-live="assertive"`, for the default foreground toast. The toast now posts a VoiceOver announcement as it appears: its title, and its description on the next line. It is said over what VoiceOver is saying rather than queued behind it (`accessibilitySpeechQueueAnnouncement` false), as an assertive region is, the way `KozmosMapStatusPill` posts its assertive words. A toast that is not shown says nothing. The look, the timing and the initializers are unchanged. `KozmosToastAnnouncementTests` records what the toast posts.
- **A POI detail icon whose artwork fails to load falls back to its name (GAP-178):** a service's, a group item's or a tag's `iconUrl` that was unsafe fell back to its `iconName` (or `systemImage`) already, but one that failed to load left no icon. The owner's rule (21 Oct) is Compose's, on all three platforms: an unsafe or failed `iconUrl` falls back to the `iconName`'s icon, and a summary fact to its kind's symbol. `POIDetailIcon` now draws that fallback once `AsyncImage` reports a failure; while the asset loads it draws nothing, as before, and with no name or symbol the label stands alone. `KozmosPOIDetailIconFallbackTests` fails the load through a URL protocol and reads the drawn icon.
- **The stepper says which step is current:** VoiceOver walked `KozmosStepper`'s circles and then its names as separate words ("Selected", "2", "3", "Search", "Route", "Go"), with nothing of which step was current. "Selected" was the checkmark symbol's own name, read on every done step. React's stepper now marks its current step `aria-current="step"`. Each step is now one element, its number and name together ("2, Route"), and the current step is selected, the platform's word for the current item of a set, as the breadcrumb's current page is. The check is decoration, as React's is, so a done step is read by its name. The look and the initializer are unchanged. `KozmosStepperAccessibilityTests` reads the tree with accessibility automation on.
- **The batching analytics modifier delivers events as React's `AnalyticsProvider` does:** `.kozmosAnalytics(batchDelayMs:onDispatch:)` never called `onDispatch`. It put a `KozmosAnalytics` engine in the environment as an `environmentObject`, which no part reads: every part sends to the `\.kozmosAnalytics` tracker. It now puts a tracker that feeds its engine in `\.kozmosAnalytics` for the view it modifies, so every part inside sends to it, and it keeps one engine for as long as that view is in the hierarchy (it made a new one, with an empty queue, each time the view above redrew).
  - **A batch is delivered `batchDelayMs` after its first event** (2000 by default), with every event sent meanwhile, in the order sent, as React's timer runs. The engine restarted its timer on every event, which made it a debounce: one event a second for five seconds was delivered once, at six. An event after a delivery opens the next batch.
  - **What is waiting when the view leaves the hierarchy is delivered then,** as React's provider delivers on unmount, and so is what a `KozmosAnalytics` of your own holds when it is released. Its `deinit` captured `[weak self]`, which is already nil there, so those events were dropped.
  - A nested modifier takes the events of the parts inside it, and the one above it gets none of them, as a nested React provider does. A tracker set inside it with `.environment(\.kozmosAnalytics, …)` takes its own subtree's events in the same way. Like React, it does not deliver early when the app moves to the background.
  - `onDispatch` is called on the main thread. `trackEvent` and `flushQueue()` act at once on the main thread, and from another thread go through the main queue; `flushQueue()` delivered later on the main queue even when called there. With no `onDispatch`, a `KozmosAnalytics` prints each event of a batch as the default tracker prints it, as React's provider logs a batch it has nowhere to send; it dropped them. The engine no longer publishes a change for each event, so a view that observes it no longer redraws for each one.
  - The signatures are unchanged. The engine stays in the environment as an `environmentObject`, so a product's own events can join the batches through `@EnvironmentObject var analytics: KozmosAnalytics` and `trackEvent`. `KozmosAnalyticsBatchingTests` runs the engine on a clock the test moves by hand, and `KozmosAnalyticsProviderTests` presses a real `KozmosCheckbox` and `KozmosSwitch` inside the modifier. The AnalyticsProvider page in Storybook (Foundations/Analytics) is new, with its SwiftUI tab compiled in the test target.
- **POI detail tags draw Kozmos icons:** a service's tag in `KozmosPOIDetailPanel` draws its `iconName` through `KozmosIcon`, as the web's chip draws `Icon` and Compose's draws the Pointr outline. `KozmosPOIDetailTag(service:)` turned the name into an SF Symbol name and the tag drew that with `Image(systemName:)`, so it drew the `questionmark.circle` stand-in for every name SF Symbols has nothing for: the 19 Pointr outlines `KozmosIcon` draws (arrow-down, arrow-up, bookmark, calendar-check-01, clock-plus, compass-01, eye, feather, flip-backward, globe-02, heart, layout-alt-02, loading-01, mail-01, phone, share-01, shopping-bag-02, stars-01 and switch-vertical-01), bluetooth-off and the 23 wayfinding glyphs. A name Kozmos does not know drew the stand-in too; it now draws no icon, only the label, as on the web and in Compose. Go's `navigation-pointer-01` draws Pointr's pointer, as `KozmosIcon` does, not the `location.north` symbol. A name SF Symbols has a symbol for draws exactly as before: 16pt, at the regular weight, in the label's colour. `KozmosPOIDetailTagIconTests` measures each name's tag in light and dark, left to right and right to left.
  - **Added: `KozmosPOIDetailTag.iconName`**, a Kozmos icon name or an alias for one, and `iconName:` in its initializer, after `label:` and defaulting to nil, so a call written before compiles and draws as it did. A tag decoded from JSON keeps the contract's `iconName`, as the web and Compose spell a group's item or a tag; it was dropped. A tag draws `iconUrl`, else `iconName` where Kozmos knows the name, else `systemImage`. To draw a Kozmos icon in a tag you build, pass its name, `iconName: "phone"`, rather than an SF Symbol in `systemImage:`, which still works.
- **A service's POI detail tag draws the service's own artwork:** `KozmosPOIDetailPanel` draws a service's `iconUrl`, tinted to the label's colour when `iconMonochrome` is set, before its `iconName`, as the web's and Compose's chips do. `KozmosPOIDetailTag(service:)` kept only the name and dropped `iconUrl` and `iconMonochrome`, so a service with its own asset drew its Kozmos name, or no icon, on iOS only. A service without an `iconUrl` draws as before. `KozmosPOIDetailTests.testAServiceTagKeepsTheServicesArtwork` holds the tag to the service's three fields.
- **`KozmosIcon` draws every Kozmos icon name:** all 80 names on the Icons page, the web registry's 57 and the 23 wayfinding glyphs, draw their own artwork, as they do on the web and in Compose. 18 drew the `questionmark.circle` stand-in an unknown name gets: arrow-down, arrow-up, bookmark, calendar-check-01, clock-plus, eye, feather, flip-backward, globe-02, heart, layout-alt-02, loading-01, mail-01, phone, share-01, shopping-bag-02, stars-01 and switch-vertical-01. compass-01 drew nothing: its SF Symbol, `compass`, is not one iOS has. The 19 now draw Pointr's own outlines, generated from `packages/icons` by `pnpm icons:native:generate` (which Compose's 18 come from too), stroked 2 on the 24 grid at any size, in the icon's colour and never mirrored, as `bluetooth-off` is. `KozmosIconNamesTests` holds `KozmosIcon` to the web registry's names and aliases, as Compose's `IconNamesTest` does. The Icon page's SwiftUI example names `bell-01`, which draws a bell, not `bell`, which is not a Kozmos name and drew the stand-in.
- **The colour picker's format row fits a phone's width:** the open `KozmosColorPicker`'s row of the format menu, the value boxes and the opacity box was an HStack whose boxes could not shrink below 328pt. With the panel's padding the open picker needed 352pt, so on a 375pt phone with 16pt margins (343pt) it grew 9pt past its frame, and 32pt at 320. Where the boxes do not fit on one line, the row now wraps as React's format grid does, `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(3.5rem, 1fr))`: equal columns, at least 56pt (96 for HEX) and at least as wide as the widest box's text, as many to a line as fit. At 343 that is five 59pt boxes on one line; at 320, four on the first line and the opacity box at the start of a second. Where the row fitted before with every box's text on one line — from 352pt at the default text size — it is laid out as before: frame for frame at 359, 390 and 430 at the default, xxxLarge and first accessibility sizes, in both directions and both appearances.
  - At accessibility text sizes the row wraps rather than squeezing its text. A value box stopped at 72pt, so its digits stood one above another, the menu's label became an ellipsis and "100%" broke across lines, at every width; the menu now grows taller with its label rather than staying 36pt. The boxes, their words and what VoiceOver reads are unchanged.
- **The colour picker's square keeps its handle on its colour right to left:** `KozmosColorPicker`'s saturation and lightness square is drawn left to right in either reading direction, white at the left and the hue at the right, as React draws it, but its handle was placed by a top-leading alignment and an offset, which mirror right to left. So right to left the handle stood at the wrong end: a fully saturated colour's handle sat over the white. The square is now laid out left to right, so the handle sits over its colour in both directions, and VoiceOver reads the same saturation and lightness. Left to right it draws exactly as before, and the swatch, the open button and the sliders still follow the reading direction.
- **A Tag's remove button is named in the product's words:** `KozmosTag` takes `removeLabel` ("Remove" by default, as React's), before `onRemove`, so a trailing closure still compiles. VoiceOver read the symbol's own "Close". Its 24pt target is unchanged.
- **A CJK category name gets three lines (decision 78, GAP-010):** `KozmosCategoryTile` gives a name in Han, kana or Hangul a third line, so a long word such as カスタマーサービス shows whole rather than cut; other scripts keep two.
- **The map shell's panel ends its content 16 above its edge (GAP-085):** `KozmosAdaptiveMapShell` leaves 16pt below the panel's content, as it does above it. In a side panel the content ends 16 above the panel's bottom edge; in the docked sheet, 16 above the bottom safe area, with the sheet's surface still reaching the screen's edge. It ran to the edge: a two-result list ended against the panel's bottom edge. A fitted sheet and a fitted side panel are 16pt taller.
  - A `KozmosPanelScrollView` still runs under that space, and under the home indicator, to the edge while it scrolls, and scrolled to its end its last row stops 16 above it. It no longer ignores the safe area the shell gives it: that took its content inset away, and a long list's last row stopped on the home indicator.
  - The content is told the space, `kozmosPanelInsetBottom` (16 in the shell, 0 outside), as it is told `kozmosPanelInsetTop`. `KozmosPOIDetailPanel` in its sheet presentation, `KozmosBrowseCategoriesPanel` on the panel's surface and `KozmosRoutePreviewPanel` top their own 16 up to it rather than adding to it, so they end where they did. A product that puts one of them above a row of its own sets it to zero for it.
- **Tabs say which tab is selected:** VoiceOver hears the selected `KozmosTabsTrigger` as selected, and only that one, as React's `aria-selected` tells a screen reader (WCAG 4.1.2). `KozmosTabsList` is a container that keeps each tab its own element and, from iOS 17, a tab bar, as React's `tablist`. Before, every tab was a plain button and the selected one was shown only by its segment. Each tab is still a button that a double tap chooses. The look, the initializers and the 44pt target are unchanged.
- **Disabled parts are dimmed once:** SwiftUI's plain button style already draws a disabled button at half (measured on iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.5, and on a Mac), and these parts set their own opacity on top of it, so they were dimmed twice.
  - Now at 50%, as React and Compose draw them, from 25%: `KozmosNavigationItem`, `KozmosCategoryTile`, `KozmosRouteOptionCard`, `POIDetailPanel`'s actions, a disabled `KozmosChip`'s remove mark, and a `KozmosPOIResultCard`'s disabled outline action.
  - A disabled `KozmosSegmentedControl` draws each part at 50%, as Compose draws the control: its track on the page, and its segments and their words on the track. The segments and words were at 25%.
  - A disabled `KozmosFloorSelector` level and a stepper chevron with nowhere to step are at 40%, as Compose's are; they were at 20%. An unavailable `KozmosPOIResultCard` row is at 60%, as React's is; it was at 30%.
  - A disabled or loading `KozmosMapControlButton` is drawn at 50% as one. Its mark was at 25%, and its surface, four copies one over another, at 68% tinted and 94% filled.
  - The tinted map control, the chip's remove button, the floor selector's levels and steppers and the result's row keep the plain style's press and pointer hover, without its disabled dimming. A result's outline action takes the borderless style, the press every other outline `KozmosButton` has. Every disabled part still takes no touch, and VoiceOver still hears it as dimmed. The initializers are unchanged.
- **POI details summary tones:** a fact's value and icon use the emotion's text role for success, warning and danger (`semanticsEmotionSuccessText`, `semanticsEmotionAlertText`, `semanticsEmotionDangerText`) and `primitivesColorsTheme600` for brand, as the web's summary names them. Each now reads at 4.5:1 or more on the card's white and the sheet's grey in both appearances. The fill colours they used read as low as 1.57:1 (warning on the sheet), and brand read at 3.74:1 on black.
- **Map credits are never clipped (GAP-135, decision 58):** `KozmosAdaptiveMapShell` no longer shrinks the attribution into a scroll region when an opened top-bar card and the sheet leave it too little room. The Pointr logo gives way first: `KozmosMapAttribution` leaves out its brand and the credits keep their full height. The panel's sizing still reserves the whole attribution (at most half the band below the top bar), so the logo giving way never moves the sheet. The room counts the lift above the bottom corners when they leave no 128pt middle slot. Credits still too tall to sit above the corners send the corners away (hidden, not hit-testable and hidden from VoiceOver, as when they do not fit), and the credits return to the bottom row. The logo comes back once it fits again: while it has given way, the shell measures an unseen, unreachable copy of the whole attribution.
- **Arrows follow the reading direction:** `KozmosPaginationPrevious` and `KozmosPaginationNext`, `KozmosBreadcrumb`'s separator and `KozmosRoutePreviewPanel`'s Back draw `chevron.backward`, `chevron.forward` and `arrow.backward`, which SwiftUI mirrors right to left. They pointed left or right in either direction.
- **Progress takes the theme:** `KozmosProgress` tints its bar with `primitivesColorsTheme600` through `.tint`, as React's bar is drawn. It drew the system blue, whatever the theme, through the deprecated `LinearProgressViewStyle(tint:)`.
- **The default analytics tracker prints the event:** with no tracker in `\.kozmosAnalytics`, every event printed `[Kozmos Analytics] \(event.component) -> \(event.eventName)`, those words, because the string escaped its interpolations. It prints the component and the event, `[Kozmos Analytics] LocationPin -> location_pin_clicked`, the line Compose's `LocalKozmosAnalytics` default prints.

### Added and changed

- **Code Connect maps the library icons:** every `Icon / *` component on the Icons page, the 80 React maps, reads in Dev Mode as `KozmosIcon("<name>")` with `import Kozmos`, a Pointr Maps - Express wayfinding glyph by its name too. An icon nested in a set read back as React's `<Icon name=…/>` with no import, because SwiftUI mapped none of them. `Sources/Components/Icon/IconLibrary.figma.swift` is generated with React's by `pnpm figma:iconconnect`; nothing a product calls changes.
- **LocationPin draws the SDK's map result markers (decisions 71, 79–81, GAP-120):** `KozmosLocationPin` draws one of three markers, measured against the MAP-474 boards, from Kozmos's tokens in both appearances.
  - **Result**, the default: a 32pt pill, at least 32 wide, with `number` (14pt semibold, tabular figures) 8 in from each side, or an 8pt dot with no number, and a 12 × 7 pointer whose tip is 6 below the pill. At rest it is background/0 inside a 2pt theme-600 border, the number theme 600; `selected` fills it with theme 600 under a foreground/1000 number. Selected fills; it no longer grows.
  - **Featured** (`featured`): a 44pt tile (52 selected), radius 16, with a 3pt foreground/0 rim on background/0, and a 6pt foreground/0 dot 4 below. `markerContent` is the tile's logo and nothing else: scaled to fit inside the rim, never cropped, clipped to the inner radius 13. With no logo the tile is empty. No number, no star and no accent colour (decision 81). VoiceOver hears `label`, then `featuredLabel`.
  - **Place**, the new `place: Bool = false`: a place on its own, its details open. It is a 40pt teardrop in emotional success 800 with a 20pt background/0 disc, and a 6pt dot 6 below. It wins over `featured` and `number`, `selected` changes nothing, and VoiceOver hears it as selected.
  - The md shadow (`semanticsElevationFloating`) falls from the body only (the pill and its pointer, the tile, the teardrop), never from a dot.
  - **The anchor** is the pointer's tip on a result and the dot's centre otherwise. The marker lays itself out with the anchor at the centre of its frame (`KozmosLocationPin.anchor`), so `.kozmosLocationPinAt(point)`, new, puts the anchor on the map's point in either direction (`.position(point)` lands on the mirrored point in right-to-left). The frame is taller than the drawing, and `externalLabel` is drawn outside it, 4pt from the marker, so it never moves the anchor.
  - **Pressing:** a tap now runs `onSelect`; before, only VoiceOver could. The target is at least 44pt square, centred on the body. The new `onOpen: (() -> Void)? = nil` opens the place's details on a double tap, as a result card's does (`KozmosDoubleTapGate`): the first tap selects at once, a second within 0.3 s opens and is not a second `onSelect`, and a third quick tap does nothing. With `onOpen`, VoiceOver offers an action named by the new `openLabel: String = "Open details"`; its own double tap is one activation, which selects.
  - **Naming (Olcay, 15 Oct 2026):** `label` is the marker's whole spoken name, as the product writes it, its number included ("Result 3, Burger King"), and it is no longer drawn. Kozmos adds nothing to it but `featuredLabel` on a featured tile ("Burger King, Featured"). The new `externalLabel: String? = nil` is the words drawn beside the marker at `labelPlacement`, as React's and Compose's are; without it nothing is drawn.
  - **`externalLabel`** is drawn beside the marker at `labelPlacement`. `.left` and `.right` are the map's left and right, physical in every language, as MapLibre places a symbol's text; `.top` and `.bottom` are unchanged. In a right-to-left layout a `.right` label used to draw on the left.
  - **Analytics:** a tap sends `location_pin_clicked` (with `kind`, `number` and `selected`) and an open `location_pin_opened` (with `kind` and `number`) through `\.kozmosAnalytics`, as React and Compose send them.
  - A marker with an action (`onSelect` or `onOpen`) is a button, and an image without one. A disabled marker is drawn at half once, as a whole, and takes no touch; with an action it is a disabled button, which VoiceOver hears as dimmed, as React's aria-disabled button and Compose's disabled button are.
  - The number is written as the result card's tab writes it, `"12"`, with no grouping separator.
  - **In production, the map draws these markers** from its style's sprite sheet, and featured tiles with their logos from a featured sprite Pointr's back end generates. Kozmos draws the same markers for prototypes and boards.
  - Code Connect maps the LocationPin set's new axes: Kind (Result, Featured, Place), State (Default, Selected) and Content (Number or Dot on a Result, None on a Featured tile or a Place), with Number Text and Label Text.
- **A double tap opens a result (decision 72, GAP-143):** `KozmosPOIResultList`, `KozmosPOIResultGroup` and `KozmosPOIResultCard` take `onOpen: ((String) -> Void)? = nil`, told the POI's ID, as React's `onOpen` is.
  - A double tap on a result, selected or not, a group's branch included, calls it once. The first tap still selects at once, with no wait; the second, on the same result within 0.3 s, opens it and is not a second `onSelect`, so the first tap's selection stands. A third quick tap does nothing. A tap after a pause, or on another result, selects. An unavailable result neither selects nor opens.
  - A VoiceOver double tap is one activation, which selects. With `onOpen`, VoiceOver offers an action named by the new `openLabel: String = "Open details"` that opens the result, on the row spoken in the interface's voice and on the one spoken in its name's language alike. Without `onOpen` there is no such action.
  - Analytics: an open is reported as `poi_result_opened` with `poiId` and `resultIndex`, as React reports it, and not as a selection.
  - Without `onOpen` every tap selects, as before. Both parameters come after the released ones, with defaults, so existing calls compile; on the list they come before the `emptyState` builder.
- **ManoeuvreCard is tighter, its grab bar lower and quieter (decision 74, GAP-140):**
  - Below the instruction row come the card's 12pt, the 36 × 5 grip and 4pt to the card's bottom edge, 21pt in all, as an iOS sheet's grabber sits. The bar's row is the grip alone: it was a 44pt row with the grip in its middle, 60pt below the content, about 50pt of it empty. Open, a fixed 23pt more lies under the itinerary (below). The closed card is 39pt shorter, the open card 16pt shorter.
  - The bar's activation area stays 44pt tall: it lies over the card and reaches up from its bottom edge over the 4pt, the grip, the 12pt and 23pt more, and wins there. Closed, those 23pt are the foot of the instruction row, which opens the card too. Open, they are a fixed space between the itinerary and the 12pt, outside its scroll view, scrolling or not: the itinerary's viewport ends 44pt above the card's edge, so the bar never covers a row at any scroll position, and a tap or a drag low in the list, a destination's Change button among it, reaches the itinerary.
  - On the theme appearance the grip is the guidance foreground at 62%, 3.09:1 on `#135BEC` (WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 for a control's only visual), where it was full white, as loud as the instruction. On the background appearance it stays background/300 at full strength.
  - The bar's name, VoiceOver's order and focus, and what it does are unchanged.
- **Featured is the accent (decisions 67, 68):**
  - `KozmosPOIResultCard`'s Featured tab and a featured card's edge draw `semanticsAccentFill` / `semanticsAccentOnfill`. That is `#FAB735` under black by default, in both appearances and both presentations. They drew the alert fill pair (`#F9A707` light, `#FBC459` dark).
  - `KozmosLocationPin` takes `markerContent: AnyView? = nil`, the place's logo, and `featuredLabel: String = "Featured"`, which replaces the hard-coded "Featured" in what VoiceOver says. How a featured pin is drawn is now the SDK's featured tile, with no accent (decisions 79 and 81, below).
  - Both parameters come last, with defaults, so existing calls compile.
  - Migration: review screenshots with featured cards. MapStatusPill's Turn Back stays on the alert colours.
- **SplitButton's menu half keeps the system press (decision 64):** a deliberate platform difference, not a gap. `KozmosSplitButton`'s menu half is a `Menu`, which does not run a custom button style's pressed state, so it draws SwiftUI's own press. The action half draws the themed pressed token, `#0D44C2`, as React and Compose draw both halves.
- **The secondary button's ink is the neutral token's (decision 61):** `KozmosButton` and `KozmosIconButton` with `.secondary` draw their words and mark in `componentsPrimaryButtonsNeutralButtonForegroundContent*` in every state: black in light and white in dark, as React and Compose do. They drew `primitivesColorsForeground100`, `#17191C` and `#E8E6E3`. The fill was already the neutral token.
- **Emotion states step away from the page (decision 60):** a filled emotion part's hover and focus are one step, and its pressed two steps, further along the emotion's own ramp: darker in light, lighter in dark. This covers `KozmosButton`, `KozmosIconButton` and every part drawn through `KozmosFillButtonStyle`.
  - Danger in light is `#B01736`, then `#8C132B`, then `#670E20` (the hover was `#D41C42`, lighter than the fill).
  - In dark, danger, success, alert and informative step lighter, 700 → 800 → 900. Light neutral presses to `#9095A2`, where it went lighter (`#E3E4E8`).

  - The colours come from `KozmosColors`, which `pnpm tokens:native:copy` regenerated.

- **Tabs draw React's raised segment (decision 65):**

## 0.6.0 — repository snapshot

Native source changes since the repository snapshot tagged `@kozmos-ds/react@0.5.0`. This entry does not announce a 0.6.0 tag or completed release validation. The web package has its own [changelog](../react/CHANGELOG.md).

### Added and changed

- **Map status pill:** `KozmosMapStatusPill` provides neutral, progress, success, danger and warning tones, optional custom marks, and off/polite/assertive announcement policies. Warning uses the named alert fill/on-fill pair. Words wrap instead of being cut; the default minimum height is 48pt. The product supplies the status text and chooses when it changes.
- **Travel-time bands:** `KozmosTravelTimeBand(durationSeconds:)` classifies a non-negative finite duration as Nearby, 1–2, 2–5, 5–10 or more than 10 minutes. Result cards use a band only when it is supplied on the travel estimate, with Nearby in the success colour. Without a band they retain the exact duration label; details continue to show the exact duration.
- **Result-card numbering:** `numbered` opts a card or list into displaying `resultIndex` in an inside-card, top-leading tab. The tab follows layout direction and leaves clearance above the title. Featured takes precedence over a number, and a number over a badge. Numbers are quiet at rest and filled when selected; Featured uses amber with dark words and a star; badges use a quiet grey treatment. An unselected Featured card has an amber edge; the selected card's theme-colour edge takes precedence.
- **Numbered map pins:** a non-Featured numbered pin on the current floor is outlined at rest and filled when selected. Off-floor pins retain a dashed outline, including when selected. Selected pins also grow. Selection is exposed in accessibility traits even when the pin is disabled or has no selection callback; this does not make it actionable.
- **Floor switcher:** the existing `.collapsible` variant now uses the SDK-style tile and expanding level column. `userFloor`, `userFloorLabel` and `expandHint` support a visitor-level marker and product-supplied wording. Floor IDs remain selection keys; display labels do not replace them.
- **Location state:** `KozmosUserLocationState.headingPaused` represents heading remembered while the map is away from the visitor. `KozmosMapControlsGroup` renders its distinct paused-heading mark and description. Map-state transitions remain the product's responsibility.
- **Map controls and navigation:** map controls use a 48pt base surface, no border in either theme, and the map-control elevation role. Rail labels use scalable `caption2` text on nominal 14pt lines, with a two-line limit, and the rail adopts the side-menu treatment.
- **Hosted panels:** browse and route-preview components reuse the map shell panel's surface instead of painting another fill. Their first rows account for the panel's existing top inset and grip clearance. Detail-header buttons align to the side inset where space permits, retaining extra clearance when they would collide with the grip target.
- **Glass text:** the public `EnvironmentValues.kozmosSurfaceStyle` signal describes the surrounding Kozmos surface. Participating muted text switches to foreground ink on glass. Standalone browse surfaces retain their own fill.
- **Manoeuvre disclosure:** `KozmosManoeuvreCard` now displays the full instruction by default; `instructionLines` opts into truncation. Disclosure changes request accessibility focus on the replacement part only when focus belonged to the part being removed.
- **Icons:** `KozmosIcon("bluetooth-off")` uses the Pointr outline. Status-pill examples use an explicit walking mark for walking guidance; progress still defaults to the spinner.

### Migration notes

- Add `.headingPaused` to exhaustive switches over `KozmosUserLocationState`; its raw value is `"heading-paused"`. Handle the location button's existing callback in the product to resume heading. Kozmos does not perform that SDK transition itself.
- Pass floors in the desired display order. The selector **does not sort** them. Supply highest-to-lowest order if the up chevron should mean a higher physical level; `userFloor` marks a level without selecting it.
- Set `numbered: true` only when the list should match numbered map markers. Supply matching, one-based `resultIndex` values yourself; cards do not assign or renumber them. A custom `selectionLabel` replaces the generated accessible description, so include the number in that label if needed.
- Set `travelEstimate.band` explicitly to opt into banded result text. Supply translated `travelTimeBandLabels`; the helper classifies seconds, not already-rounded labels. Exactly 2, 5 and 10 minutes remain in the lower band; negative or non-finite durations produce `nil`.
- Pass `instructionLines: 2` to retain the old manoeuvre instruction limit. Without it, allow the surrounding layout to grow. A limit under one also means no limit.
- Review screenshot baselines for tabs, pins, control surfaces, rail labels, hosted fills and header spacing. Native selected-card edges are not the web's separate selection ring.
- Rebuild consuming apps against the exact source revision and review their initializer calls. This candidate does **not** guarantee all 0.5.0 native call sites remain source-compatible; the broader native compatibility follow-up is not included.

### Limits and validation scope

- This source review does not establish human VoiceOver behaviour. The manoeuvre disclosure requests focus on the itinerary container when opening and the instruction when closing; verify the resulting experience with the product's actual itinerary and VoiceOver on a device.
- Glass uses the system material plus Kozmos tint and edge. Its blur and saturation are system-defined, not pixel-identical to the web. Reduce Transparency uses a solid surface; the stronger glass-text treatment remains.
- Dedicated Turn Back U-turn and Wayfinding Unavailable marks are not supplied here. Use an appropriate custom icon, or `showsIcon: false`; do not treat the default warning triangle as those dedicated marks.
- The iOS harness is configured to run the complete test target on a pinned simulator. A macOS `swift test` result alone does not validate iOS-only tests, and this changelog is not proof that the final candidate passed that simulator run.
- Held follow-ups [#160](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/160), [#164](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/164), [#166](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/166) and [#168](https://github.com/vodoco/kozmos-design-system/pull/168) are outside this candidate. Their proposed fixes are not claimed here.
