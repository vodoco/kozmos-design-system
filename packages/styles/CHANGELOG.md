### Minor Changes

- 2e768c2: Button takes `variant="subtle"`, CardHeader takes `tone="subtle"` and CardTitle takes `size` (GAP-200, CLOUD-UI-001, GAP-035; decisions 132, 135 and 139).
  - **`variant="subtle"` on Button**: a pale fill of its own token tier, `Components.Subtle Buttons`, new in `@kozmos-ds/tokens` in both themes. Unset, it is theme 0 under theme 600, the theme as text (decision 59): 5.72:1 in light and 4.80:1 in dark. Hovered and focused it is theme 100 under theme 700, and pressed theme 200 under theme 800: its words step with its fill, because theme 600 on theme 100 reads only 4.41:1. Every state is a step of the theme ramp, so a client's ramp carries it. With `emotion` it is that emotion's pale fill under its words; `emotion="danger"` is the destructive tone of Pointr Cloud's Delete Feature, danger 0 under the danger text (decision 139). In dark a pressed `alert` keeps its hover's fill, as no step of the dark alert ramp reads 4.5:1 on its 200. The tier has no disabled tokens of its own. Its class sets the themed emotion's custom properties itself, so `buttonVariants({ variant: "subtle" })` draws it on a link too, and an emotion's inline properties replace them. A subtle SplitButton's seam is the fill's pressed step, as the filled variants' white at a fifth does not show on it. Full width is `className="w-full"`, as for every Button. IconButton, FloatingActionButton, MapControlButton and SplitButton take Button's props, so their `variant` types list `subtle` too.
  - **An emotion's filled Button now takes its words from each state's token** (`--kz-button-fg-focus` and `--kz-button-fg-pressed` join the custom properties an emotion sets). The Primary tier's words are one colour in every state, so nothing it draws changes; the subtle tier's step.
  - **`tone="subtle"` on CardHeader**: Pointr Cloud's form side panel header, a pale theme strip, theme 0, full-bleed to the card's edges and inside its top corners, at least 52 tall and taller as its content needs (its top corners the card's only when it is the card's first child), 12 above and below its words and the card's padding at its sides, with the card's padding below the tint before the content. With `actions`, its title and actions are centred in it. Its title, description and a ghost action's ink read 4.5:1 on it in both themes.
  - **`size` on CardTitle** (the owner's ruling of 9 Nov): `lg` 24, the default, as it was; `md` 18 for a compact card on a phone or a dashboard's section card; `sm` 14, as `sm` is 14 on Text. Unset, a title in a subtle header is `sm`, the strip's. With `actions`, they are centred on the smaller title's line. The heading level stays `as`'s.

- 16865be: Container takes `block`, its top and bottom space, and `PageHeader` is new (GAP-116, GAP-036; decisions 132 and 135).
  - **`block` on Container** (GAP-116): the clear space above and below the content, `"sm"` 16, `"md"` 24 or `"lg"` 32 (the layout spacing steps 200, 300 and 400), or `{ start, end }` for the top and the bottom apart, so a page column under a top bar takes its own space rather than a Box around it. Unset or `"none"`, a Container pads its sides only, as it always has; `inset` is unchanged. The space holds whatever the window does, and a caller's own `pt-*` or `pb-*` still wins. `ContainerBlock` and `ContainerBlockInset` are exported.
  - **`PageHeader` is new** (GAP-036, decision 135): the top of a page, one part for every page. `title` is the page's one `h1`; `breadcrumb` sits above it, `info` (an info button) on its first line after it, `description` under it in the muted small text, and `actions` at the end of the title's line, centred on it, wrapping under the text where the page is too narrow for both (at the left in right to left). `tone="subtle"`, the default, paints a band across the page in theme 0, CardHeader's subtle strip's tint; `tone="default"` paints none. `size="md"`, the default, draws the title at Heading 3's 24/32, and `size="lg"` at Heading 2's 30/36, for a landing page. Its content sits in a Container with `block={{ start: "lg", end: "md" }}`, 32 above and 24 below, and takes Container's `inset` and `centered`. A slot not given draws nothing.

  SwiftUI's `KozmosContainer(inset:block:)` takes `KozmosContainerBlock` (`.sm`, `.md`, `.lg`, or `.init(start:end:)`) and Compose's `KozmosContainer(inset, block = KozmosContainerBlock.Sm)` the same; both have `KozmosPageHeader`, with the title a heading, the band, the two sizes and the breadcrumb, info, description and actions slots (their actions wrap under the text where they don't fit beside it, as React's do, and an actions slot that draws nothing takes no room). The Figma importer paints Container's Block axis and a PageHeader set. Vue's bridge exports `KozmosPageHeader`, and `KozmosContainer` takes `block`.

- f88f347: `Kbd`, `ContextMenu`, and `external` on menu items (GAP-156, GAP-155, GAP-202; decisions 132 and 152).
  - **`Kbd` is new.** A key drawn as a key cap, as a `<kbd>`: `<Kbd>Esc</Kbd>`, or a combination, `<Kbd keys={["Ctrl", "C"]} />`, drawn as a `<kbd>` of `<kbd>`s joined by "+". Each key is drawn as written and read by its name, so "Press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to clear" is read "Press Escape to clear": Esc, Ctrl, Cmd, Opt, Del, Bksp, PgUp, PgDn, Ins and Win, and the symbols ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃ ↵ ⏎ ⌫ ⌦ ⇥ ↑ ↓ ← → ␣ ⇪, have English names; any other key is read as written. `label` replaces what is read with the product's own words. `kbdKeyName(key)` gives a key's name. The name is never drawn or copied: copying a hint copies what it shows, "Press Esc to clear", on one line. One size; it draws the glyph it is given and swaps no ⌘ for Ctrl by platform.
  - **`ContextMenu` is new**, on `@radix-ui/react-context-menu` (a new dependency): `ContextMenu`, `ContextMenuTrigger`, `ContextMenuContent`, `ContextMenuItem`, `ContextMenuCheckboxItem`, `ContextMenuRadioItem`, `ContextMenuLabel`, `ContextMenuSeparator`, `ContextMenuShortcut`, `ContextMenuGroup`, `ContextMenuPortal`, `ContextMenuSub`, `ContextMenuSubContent`, `ContextMenuSubTrigger` and `ContextMenuRadioGroup`. It is Menu's panel and rows, with Menu's classes, opened at the pointer by a right click, by a long press on touch, and at the focused item's inline start and block end by the keyboard's context-menu key or Shift+F10, in every browser; a `contextmenu` event inside the open menu (Chromium on Windows fires one on the menu key's keyup) is cancelled, so the browser's own menu never opens over it. A modal one makes the page inert while it is open, as Menu does (GAP-193).
  - **Menu's and ContextMenu's items take `external`** (decision 152), as Link does: the new-tab icon at the item's inline end, mirrored right to left, and `externalLabel` ("opens in a new tab") read after its name. Around a caller's `<a>` (`asChild`) it adds `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, the caller's own winning, and a `target` that keeps the page in this tab draws and says nothing of a new one.

  SwiftUI has `KozmosKbd`, `.kozmosContextMenu(_:onItemSelect:)` (the system's context menu with KozmosMenu's rows) and `external` on `KozmosMenuItem`; Compose has `KozmosKbd`, `KozmosContextMenuArea` (its content keeps its own focus stops; `Modifier.kozmosContextMenuTarget()` gives an element in it TalkBack's "Show menu" action and opens the menu at it; with nothing marked, the area is one stop named by `label`, "More options" by default) and `external` on `KozmosMenuItem`. The Figma importer paints a Kbd set, a ContextMenu set and an External property on Menu's items. Vue's bridge exports `KozmosKbd`, the whole part, and `KozmosContextMenu`, which is only ContextMenu's root, as `KozmosMenu` is only Menu's: the trigger, content and items are not bridged, so a Vue app cannot yet compose a context menu through the bridge.

- c1dfcda: A mixed value on Input, NumberInput, Select and Combobox, and a list in EmptyState (GAP-153, GAP-165; decision 132).
  - **A field can stand for several values at once (GAP-153)**, as a property of a multi-selection whose features differ. `mixed` shows none of the field's own value and "Multiple values" (`mixedLabel`) in its place, as the placeholder, which a screen reader says once with the empty field (Select's trigger, a button, is described with it instead, its drawn words kept from screen readers). It is controlled, as the selection is the product's: the visitor's first edit comes through the field's own change callback (`onChange`, `onValueChange`), and the product clears `mixed`, so the field keeps its mixed state until it is edited. `onResetMixed`, given for a field whose values differ, draws "Reset to multiple values" (`resetMixedLabel`) under the field once it is no longer mixed, never while it is disabled or read-only; pressed, focus returns to the field and the product drops the edit and sets `mixed` again. An uncontrolled Input or NumberInput drops its default value while mixed, is emptied as it turns mixed, and is emptied on reset.
    - Input, NumberInput and Combobox take the four props. A mixed Combobox checks no option and shows no choice, though the visitor may still type a query; a choice is the edit, the clear button empties only the query (it reports no empty choice), and the reset empties the text with the choice. A mixed NumberInput's steppers are disabled: a step would write one number over every feature's own. It is a numeric text field while mixed, so no key, wheel or screen-reader increment steps it either; typing edits it, and it reports a value only once the text is a number ("-", "." and "-." report nothing), keeping the typed text, a "-5" or "-0.5", until it loses focus. With a `unit` (GAP-098) the unit stays drawn while mixed and is read after "Multiple values". With an `action` (GAP-154), Input's copy is disabled while mixed: there is no value of its own to copy, and "Copied" is never said over nothing.
    - Select takes them on its root, which holds the value: mixed, it gives Radix no value, so `SelectValue` shows the mixed label and no option is checked, and `SelectTrigger` is described with it and draws the reset, never while the Select is disabled at its root or its trigger. A Select given `mixed` always gives Radix a value, so it never switches between controlled and uncontrolled: without your `value` it holds the choice, from `defaultValue` only while it is not mixed, and never shows a value it held before it turned mixed. `SelectProps` is exported, and `SelectTrigger` now always carries an `id` (yours, or one of its own) so focus can return to it.
    - `FieldWrapper` takes `mixed`, `mixedLabel`, `mixedId`, `onResetMixed` and `resetMixedLabel`, which the fields use; `MixedValueProps` is the four a field takes.
  - **EmptyState takes `list` (GAP-165):** items drawn as a bulleted list under the description, from its leading edge in the 280 column, above any progress and the action, and read in order as a list of so many items; `listLabel` names it. Each item is a line of text or the product's own inline content, such as a Link. An empty list draws nothing.

  `@kozmos-ds/styles` gains `.kozmos-field-reset`, the reset's text button, and `.kozmos-empty-state-list`. SwiftUI and Compose take the same: `mixed`, `mixedLabel`, `onResetMixed` and `resetMixedLabel` on their Input, NumberInput and Combobox, on Compose's `KozmosSelectTrigger`, and on SwiftUI's `KozmosSelect`, which gains `KozmosSelectField`, the closed field it draws from `KozmosSelect(value:placeholder:label:…)`; `list` and `listLabel` on EmptyState. The Figma importer paints State=Mixed (NumberInput's steppers disabled there), a Reset Mixed boolean (a Mixed variant has no reset layer) and Mixed Text and Reset Mixed Text on the Input, NumberInput, Combobox and Select sets, and a List boolean with List Item 1–3 Text on EmptyState; Code Connect maps them on all three platforms. Vue's bridge takes `mixed` as a boolean and `@reset-mixed`; a mixed Select cannot be drawn from Vue yet, as its trigger is not bridged (GAP-230).

- 70845bd: Radio cards and tiles, and a description on Checkbox, Switch and Radio, with Switch's state in words (GAP-158, GAP-162; decision 132).
  - **`RadioGroupItem` takes `variant`**: `"default"` (the ring and its label, as before), `"card"` or `"tile"`.
