`ComboboxOption` takes `group` (GAP-192), so Combobox, Listbox and MultiSelect list their options under their groups, as MAP-64's content type picker lists types under their families. The groups come in the order they first appear in `options`, each group's options together in their own order, under the group's label, drawn as `SelectLabel` draws its own (14/20 semibold, 6 above and below, 12 at the sides; an owned rule, `.kozmos-option-group-label`, so it holds where no `@scope` rule reaches). An option with no `group` is listed on its own, as before, and a list with no groups draws as before. Filtering keeps a group's label while any of its options matches and drops it when none does. Each group is a `role="group"` named by its label through `aria-labelledby`; the label is not an option, and the arrow keys, Home and End move through the options in the order they are drawn and never stop on a label. Filtering, the first match made active and `aria-activedescendant` are unchanged. Listbox's rows keep their 4 apart inside a group, and a press on a row in a group keeps focus on the list. In Figma, Combobox, MultiSelect and Listbox take Show Group Labels, Group 1 Text and Group 2 Text, which their Code Connect maps to `group`. SwiftUI and Compose do not group yet.
