Button takes `variant="subtle"`, CardHeader takes `tone="subtle"` and CardTitle takes `size` (GAP-200, CLOUD-UI-001, GAP-035; decisions 132, 135 and 139).

- **`variant="subtle"` on Button**: a pale fill of its own token tier, `Components.Subtle Buttons`, new in `@kozmos-ds/tokens` in both themes. Unset, it is theme 0 under theme 600, the theme as text (decision 59): 5.72:1 in light and 4.80:1 in dark. Hovered and focused it is theme 100 under theme 700, and pressed theme 200 under theme 800: its words step with its fill, because theme 600 on theme 100 reads only 4.41:1. Every state is a step of the theme ramp, so a client's ramp carries it. With `emotion` it is that emotion's pale fill under its words; `emotion="danger"` is the destructive tone of Pointr Cloud's Delete Feature, danger 0 under the danger text (decision 139). In dark a pressed `alert` keeps its hover's fill, as no step of the dark alert ramp reads 4.5:1 on its 200. The tier has no disabled tokens: a disabled Button is drawn at half, as every Button is. With no emotion given the subtle Button is drawn as the themed emotion of its tier, through the custom properties an emotion sets, so the stylesheet has no colour rule of its own for it. Full width is `className="w-full"`, as for every Button. IconButton, FloatingActionButton, MapControlButton and SplitButton take Button's props, so their `variant` types list `subtle` too.
- **An emotion's filled Button now takes its words from each state's token** (`--kz-button-fg-focus` and `--kz-button-fg-pressed` join the custom properties an emotion sets). The Primary tier's words are one colour in every state, so nothing it draws changes; the subtle tier's step.
- **`tone="subtle"` on CardHeader**: Pointr Cloud's form side panel header, a pale theme strip, theme 0, full-bleed to the card's edges and inside its top corners, at least 52 tall, 12 above and below its words and the card's padding at its sides, with the card's padding below the tint before the content. With `actions`, its title and actions are centred in it. Its title, description and a ghost action's ink read 4.5:1 on it in both themes.
- **`size` on CardTitle**: `default` 24, as it was; `sm` 18 for a compact card on a phone or a dashboard's section card; `xs` 14. Unset, a title in a subtle header is 14, the strip's. With `actions`, they are centred on the smaller title's line. The heading level stays `as`'s.
