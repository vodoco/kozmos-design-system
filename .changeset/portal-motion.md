The design config's reduced motion reaches the theme's portal. `DesignConfigProvider` marks its scope `data-kozmos-motion="reduced"` when its config reduces motion (`motion: "reduced"` or `accessibility.reduceMotion`), and every looping animation stops on that mark (GAP-50); but Popover, Dialog, Drawer and BottomSheet, Tooltip, Select, Menu and Combobox's list draw in the provider's portal, outside that scope, so a Spinner or Skeleton in one kept running. The portal of every `ThemeProvider` inside the config's provider now carries the same mark, kept in step as the config changes. Reduced motion carries downward: a provider nested inside a reduced one is reduced too, its mark and its motion scale and effects, in its portal as in place, where the rules that read the mark by ancestor already saw it so (`useDesignConfig()` still returns its own config). The theme, density, direction and tokens already reached the portal, and entry and exit animations were already cut by the config's motion scale; nothing else changes.
