`Kbd`, `ContextMenu`, and `external` on menu items (GAP-156, GAP-155, GAP-202; decisions 132 and 152).

- **`Kbd` is new.** A key drawn as a key cap, as a `<kbd>`: `<Kbd>Esc</Kbd>`, or a combination, `<Kbd keys={["Ctrl", "C"]} />`, drawn as a `<kbd>` of `<kbd>`s joined by "+". Each key is drawn as written and read by its name, so "Press <Kbd>Esc</Kbd> to clear" is read "Press Escape to clear": Esc, Ctrl, Cmd, Opt, Del, Bksp, PgUp, PgDn, Ins and Win, and the symbols ⌘ ⌥ ⇧ ⌃ ↵ ⏎ ⌫ ⌦ ⇥ ↑ ↓ ← → ␣ ⇪, have English names; any other key is read as written. `label` replaces what is read with the product's own words. `kbdKeyName(key)` gives a key's name. The name is never drawn or copied: copying a hint copies what it shows, "Press Esc to clear", on one line. One size; it draws the glyph it is given and swaps no ⌘ for Ctrl by platform.
- **`ContextMenu` is new**, on `@radix-ui/react-context-menu` (a new dependency): `ContextMenu`, `ContextMenuTrigger`, `ContextMenuContent`, `ContextMenuItem`, `ContextMenuCheckboxItem`, `ContextMenuRadioItem`, `ContextMenuLabel`, `ContextMenuSeparator`, `ContextMenuShortcut`, `ContextMenuGroup`, `ContextMenuPortal`, `ContextMenuSub`, `ContextMenuSubContent`, `ContextMenuSubTrigger` and `ContextMenuRadioGroup`. It is Menu's panel and rows, with Menu's classes, opened at the pointer by a right click, by a long press on touch, and at the focused item's inline start and block end by the keyboard's context-menu key or Shift+F10, in every browser; a `contextmenu` event inside the open menu (Chromium on Windows fires one on the menu key's keyup) is cancelled, so the browser's own menu never opens over it. A modal one makes the page inert while it is open, as Menu does (GAP-193).
- **Menu's and ContextMenu's items take `external`** (decision 152), as Link does: the new-tab icon at the item's inline end, mirrored right to left, and `externalLabel` ("opens in a new tab") read after its name. Around a caller's `<a>` (`asChild`) it adds `target="_blank"` and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, the caller's own winning, and a `target` that keeps the page in this tab draws and says nothing of a new one.

SwiftUI has `KozmosKbd`, `.kozmosContextMenu(_:onItemSelect:)` (the system's context menu with KozmosMenu's rows) and `external` on `KozmosMenuItem`; Compose has `KozmosKbd`, `KozmosContextMenuArea` (its content keeps its own focus stops; `Modifier.kozmosContextMenuTarget()` gives an element in it TalkBack's "Show menu" action and opens the menu at it) and `external` on `KozmosMenuItem`. The Figma importer paints a Kbd set, a ContextMenu set and an External property on Menu's items. Vue's bridge exports `KozmosKbd`, the whole part, and `KozmosContextMenu`, which is only ContextMenu's root, as `KozmosMenu` is only Menu's: the trigger, content and items are not bridged, so a Vue app cannot yet compose a context menu through the bridge.
