`Stepper` takes steps as objects, an orientation and step selection (GAP-173, decision 119), for Pointr Cloud's building wizard. A string step works as before.

- **Steps as objects.** `steps` takes strings and `{ label, status?, disabled? }` objects, mixed. Without a status a step follows `currentStep`: done before it, current at it, pending after it. `status` is `"done"`, `"current"`, `"pending"`, `"error"` or `"loading"`: Stepper's own words for the first three, as its dots name their states, and Kozmos's other statuses' words for the last two. `error` is the danger fill with a cross, as a danger Button is; `loading` is the current ring with an arc that turns while the step works and stands still under reduced motion, the media query's or the design config's. Every status has its own glyph or number, and done, error and loading are 3:1 or more on the page in both themes: 5.62, 6.95 and 6.24:1 in light, 3.74, 8.05 and 6.17:1 in dark.
- **What a screen reader hears.** The numbered stepper is a list named by the position, "Step 2 of 5" by default, from `positionLabel`, as the dots' group is. Each step says its status after its label, "Levels, Error"; the words are English by default, and `statusLabels` takes the product's, for one status or all five. The current step stays `aria-current="step"`, whatever its status, and its own word is not said again. The indicator is drawn for the eye only. No steps draws the root alone, as before.
- **`orientation`.** `"horizontal"`, the default, or `"vertical"`: a column, each 32 indicator 12 before its label, the steps 56 apart, with a 1 by 16 line between them, centred under the indicators, in either direction. Labels wrap in a column and truncate in a row, as before.
- **`onStepSelect(index)`.** With it, every step the visitor may go to is a button: by default the steps before the current one, and a step's `disabled` says otherwise, `true` to close an earlier step and `false` to open one ahead. The current step never is. Each button is a Tab stop of its own, as Pagination's pages and Breadcrumb's links are: a list of buttons is not a composite widget, so it takes no roving focus. A focused step draws a 2px ring. Its target is the density's control height, 44 in touch and 32 in compact, or the step as drawn where that is larger, so a button moves nothing.
- **The dots** take objects for their names and draw the position only; `orientation`, a status and `onStepSelect` are the numbered variant's. Dots with strings and `positionLabel`, as TourStep draws them, are unchanged.
- **New types:** `StepperStep`, `StepperStepStatus` and `StepperOrientation`. `StepperProps` gains `orientation`, `onStepSelect` and `statusLabels`, and `steps` takes `(string | StepperStep)[]`.

In Figma, Stepper gains an Orientation axis, Horizontal and Vertical, for Numbered (the dots are a row), and Step 1 Done to Step 8 Error booleans that give one step its own status over the one its place gives it. Update renames every variant from before the axis to Horizontal in place, keeping its node id. Code Connect for the new properties follows the Figma Update.
