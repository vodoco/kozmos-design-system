`TourStep` is new (GAP-188, decision 116): one step of a product tour, the MAP-64 card, beside the control the step points at, which Backdrop's cut-out lights while the rest of the page is dimmed. It is controlled and it is one step: the sequence, pausing and resuming stay the product's.

- **What it carries.** `tourLabel` and the position ("Step 2 of 5", from `step` and `stepCount`), a `title`, `children` (any content: text, Tags, a Table), a `hint` on the themed surface, an `instruction` (what completes the step, a polite live region beside a pulsing marker), the progress as Stepper's dots, Back (`onBack`), Next (`onNext`) and Close (`onClose`). Each button is drawn only when its handler is given, except Close. `backLabel` "Back", `nextLabel` "Next", `closeLabel` "Close" and `positionLabel` "Step {step} of {count}" (a template or a function) are English defaults (decision 100). A `step` past `stepCount` draws every dot done, for a closing card.
- **Where it sits.** `target` is an element, a ref, a virtual element or a rect, as Backdrop's is. The card sits 12 past the cut-out's ring on its `side` ("bottom") at `align` ("center"), flips where there is no room, keeps 16 from the window's edges, and is placed again every frame, so it moves with the cut-out. With no target, or one not on the page, the whole page is dimmed and the card places itself in the middle of the window.
- **Pressing outside.** Escape and Close call `onClose`; a press on the dim or on the lit target does not, and the target takes the press through the cut-out. `onInteractOutside` is called on each, for a product that wants to close there.
- **Accessibility.** A non-modal dialog named by its title and described by its tour, position, body, hint and status, it takes focus when it opens, on Next (else on itself), whether a press or the page opened it, and each `step` is drawn as a dialog of its own, so moving on is heard. Closed with focus lost, PopoverContent gives focus back (GAP-190): to what had it as the step opened, else to the step's target, and a step's Next keeps focus as the next step opens. The dots are for the eye; the position is in words.
- **The look.** Popover's surface and the Control corner, 16 inside, a 4 top edge of the theme fill, 340 wide (21.25rem), sections 12 apart, in owned CSS (logical sides, so right to left mirrors it). Above its own Backdrop whatever their order in the portal, and below a popover opened while it is open. The marker holds still under reduced motion, the system's or the config's; forced colours draw it in Highlight.
- **A single hint (coachmark).** With no `stepCount`, `onBack` or `onNext`, it is a coachmark (GAP-157): no position, no dots, closed by its button and by Escape, focus on itself. There is no separate Coachmark part.

New types: `TourStepProps`, `TourStepTarget`, `TourStepSide`, `TourStepAlign` and `TourStepOutsideEvent`. In Figma, the importer draws a `TourStep` set; SwiftUI and Compose come later (decision 116).
