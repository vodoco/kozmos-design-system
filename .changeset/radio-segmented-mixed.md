A mixed state on RadioGroup and SegmentedControl, and SegmentedControl's options shrink to their words in a narrow space (decisions 188, 191 and 201; GAP-267).

- **A RadioGroup or SegmentedControl can stand for several values at once (decision 191)**, as a property of a multi-selection whose features chose different options. `mixed` chooses no option and draws "Multiple values" (`mixedLabel`) under the options, a field's helper line. A radio has no mixed state to announce, so the line is the group's description (`aria-describedby`), said once as the group is entered. Choosing an option ends it: `onValueChange` reports the choice and the product clears `mixed`. `onResetMixed`, given for a group whose values differ, draws "Reset to multiple values" (`resetMixedLabel`) under the options once it is no longer mixed, as the fields draw it, never while it is disabled; pressed, focus goes to the group's chosen option and the product sets `mixed` again. Input, NumberInput, Select and Combobox take the same names (GAP-153). Given `mixed` at all, true or false, an uncontrolled group holds its choice itself, as Select does: `defaultValue` while it is not mixed, none while it is, the visitor's after, and never a choice it held before it turned mixed again. Without `mixed` both parts are what they were. A caller's `aria-describedby` on RadioGroup is now kept beside the error's, where it replaced it.
- **SegmentedControl's options shrink to their words when the space is short (GAP-267, decision 201).** An option keeps its width, 97, 117 or 137 by `size`, when there is room, or its words' if they are wider; short of room each gives up its spare width, down to its words and never past them, so no word is clipped or runs into the next option's, and the track scrolls only when the words alone are wider. Three small options, "Hours", "Closed" and "Always open", now fit a 296 column at about 95, 95 and 97 in Chromium, WebKit and Firefox; they took 301 and scrolled. German's "Öffnungszeiten", "Geschlossen" and "Immer geöffnet" are 113, 98 and 113 at their words and still need 334. A full-width control's options are never narrower than their words either; their text ran into the next option's. Each option is now a one-column grid holding its label in a span, and a control with room draws exactly as before.

`@kozmos-ds/styles` gains the classes these draw with. SwiftUI and Compose take the same: `mixed`, `mixedLabel`, `onResetMixed` and `resetMixedLabel` on `KozmosRadioGroup` (with `disabled` or `enabled`, for the reset) and `KozmosSegmentedControl`, and the segmented control's options lay out by the same rule; Compose's options are radios in a selectable group, and its track scrolls when the words alone are wider. The Figma importer paints State=Mixed on SegmentedControl and Checked=Mixed on Radio, nothing chosen with the "Multiple values" line and a Mixed Text property. Code Connect maps Checked=Mixed and State=Mixed to `mixed` on all three platforms; Mixed Text is mapped once the owner's plugin Update has put it in the library, as Code Connect validates against the live file. Vue's bridge takes `mixed` as a boolean and `@reset-mixed` on both; a bridged RadioGroup still cannot draw its options (GAP-230).
