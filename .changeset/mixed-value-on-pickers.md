A mixed value on MultiSelect, TimePicker, DatePicker and DateRangePicker (GAP-255, decision 188).

- **The pickers take GAP-153's mixed state**, as Input, NumberInput, Select and Combobox do: `mixed`, `mixedLabel` ("Multiple values"), `onResetMixed` and `resetMixedLabel` ("Reset to multiple values"). It is controlled, as the selection is the product's: the visitor's first edit comes through the field's own change callback, and the product clears `mixed`, so the field keeps its mixed state until it is edited. `onResetMixed` draws "Reset to multiple values" under the field once it is no longer mixed, never while it is disabled or read-only; pressed, focus returns to the field and the product drops the edit and sets `mixed` again.
  - A mixed MultiSelect shows no chips, "Multiple values" in their place as the empty field's placeholder, which a screen reader says once with it, and opening it shows no option chosen. There is nothing to clear, and Backspace removes nothing. A choice is the edit: `onValueChange` reports that one option, chosen from none, so it replaces every feature's choices, as a typed name replaces every feature's name in Input. The reset also empties the typed query and closes the list. An uncontrolled MultiSelect drops the choice it held as it turns mixed, and never shows it again.
  - A mixed TimePicker or DatePicker holds no value of its own. A date or time input draws no placeholder, so the mixed label is drawn over its empty segments, in the placeholder's colour, and hidden from screen readers, which hear it once, as the field's description; in focus, the segments come back to be typed in. The list of times opens on no time and the calendar on no day. A typed or chosen value is the edit. An uncontrolled field is emptied as it turns mixed, and on reset.
  - DateRangePicker takes one mixed state for the range: neither end shows a date, both are drawn and described with the mixed label, the calendar opens on no range, and one reset under the range hands focus to the start. A typed or chosen end is the edit, reported with the other end empty. An uncontrolled range drops the dates it held as it turns mixed.

`@kozmos-ds/styles` gains `.kozmos-temporal-mixed`, the mixed label drawn over a date or time field, and hides a mixed field's empty segments until it takes focus. SwiftUI and Compose take the same four props on `KozmosMultiSelect`, `KozmosTimePicker`, `KozmosDatePicker` and `KozmosDateRangePicker`: the system's and Material 3's pickers stay (decision 104), under the mixed label. The Figma importer paints State=Mixed, a Reset Mixed boolean (a Mixed variant has no reset layer) and Mixed Text and Reset Mixed Text on the MultiSelect, DatePicker, DateRangePicker and TimePicker sets; Mixed with Open chooses nothing. Code Connect maps them on all three platforms. Vue's bridge takes `mixed` as a boolean and `@reset-mixed` on the four.
