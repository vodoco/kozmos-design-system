`Semantics.Scrollbar.thumb` and `thumbHover` (GAP-139): the thumb of Kozmos's thin scrollbar and the thumb under the pointer, foreground 500 and 400, 3:1 on the page in both themes (in the contrast contract). CSS `--semantics-scrollbar-thumb` and `--semantics-scrollbar-thumb-hover`, and the JS, Swift, Kotlin and Figma outputs carry them.
